Looking for a hairdresser in Edmonton? This page lists hair salons across the city and nearby towns, ranked by Google rating and review count. Each listing has hours, photos, a map and recent reviews, so you can compare salons before you book.

A good stylist is worth the search. Look at photos of the salon's own work, especially colour and balayage, and read recent reviews for stylists that clients mention by name. For colour, highlights or extensions, ask for a consultation first so you get a price before the appointment. Plan your booking too: most salons listed here are closed on Sundays, and many close on Mondays.
