Alberta's family doctor shortage is real. When you need to be seen this week — for a persistent cough, a suspicious rash, or a prescription refill — a walk-in clinic is often the fastest path.

## Where to go

Medicine Place operates four clinic locations across Edmonton, each with an on-site pharmacy so you can pick up your prescription before you leave:

- **[Medicine Place Walk-In Clinic 97 St North](/medical/walk-in-clinics)** — 13514 97 St NW. Diagnostic imaging on-site (same-day X-rays), plus attached pharmacy and hearing/eye clinics.
- **Medicine Place Walk-In Clinic Downtown** — 10660 105 St NW. Transit-accessible on the LRT line.
- **Medicine Place Walk-In Clinic (5809 118 Ave)** — northeast Edmonton.
- **Medicine Place Beverly Medical Clinic** — 4243 118 Ave NW, in Beverly.

## What to bring

- Alberta Health card (or a valid provincial card)
- List of any medications you're on, including dosages
- Photo of the rash / injury if it's changed since the morning
- Payment for anything not covered (sick notes, physicals, immunization) — usually $20–$60

## Best time to go

- **Weekday mornings, 9–10 AM.** Shortest wait, freshest team.
- Avoid Mondays and Sundays — Mondays are the busiest day of the week, and many clinics have reduced Sunday staffing.
- Call ahead. Wait times swing wildly — a 5-minute call saves 90 minutes.

## For after-hours

If it's after 8 PM or the weekend, the nearest options are hospital emergency departments (Royal Alex, Grey Nuns, U of A). For non-urgent issues that can wait until the next business day, book online at any Medicine Place location and skip the walk-in queue entirely.
