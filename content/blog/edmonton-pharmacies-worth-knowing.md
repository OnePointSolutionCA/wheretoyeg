Big-box pharmacies are convenient — but community pharmacies get to know you. Same-day fills, medication reviews, compounding, and a pharmacist who remembers what you're taking.

## Locally owned pharmacies

**[Acme Drug Mart](/medical/pharmacies)** — 15508 87 Ave NW Unit 103. West-end independent serving the same neighbourhoods for years. Prescription fills, compounding, immunizations, blister packs, and free local delivery.

**[Medicine Place Pharmacies](/medical/pharmacies)** operates four community locations across Edmonton, all attached to walk-in clinics:

- **13514 97 St North** — same-day fills, compounding, immunizations, free delivery
- **4245 118 Ave (Beverly)** — pharmacy + walk-in clinic in one visit
- **5809 118 Ave NW** — same setup for northeast Edmonton
- **10660 105 St NW** — downtown transit-accessible location

## Services beyond fills

Ask about:

- **Blister packs (compliance packaging)** — pre-sorted by day and dose. Life-changing for anyone managing 5+ medications.
- **Medication reviews (annual)** — free with your Alberta Health card. A pharmacist walks through everything you're taking to spot conflicts or duplicates.
- **Immunizations** — flu, shingles, pneumonia, travel vaccines. No appointment needed at most.
- **Compounding** — custom-made medications when off-the-shelf doesn't fit (pediatric doses, allergen-free versions, etc.)

## Save on prescriptions

- Ask if the generic version is available — often 30–70% cheaper.
- If you have benefits, submit through your provider's app rather than at the counter for faster processing.
- For chronic meds, ask about 90-day fills — cuts dispensing fees roughly in third.
