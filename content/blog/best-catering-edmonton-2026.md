Picking a caterer is one of those decisions that looks simple until you're three weeks out from an event and realize the menu doesn't work, the headcount changed, and nobody asked about allergies. Edmonton has a deep bench of caterers, the trick is matching the right one to the type of event you're running.

## What to ask before you book

- **What's the minimum headcount?** Most corporate caterers start at 20. Some tiffin services do as few as 5. Know the floor.
- **Do they do setup and cleanup, or just drop off?** Full-service and drop-off are different prices and different levels of stress.
- **Dietary accommodations.** Halal, vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free. For halal, ask whether the kitchen is fully halal or just offers halal options, and how they handle cross-contamination.
- **Tasting available?** Any caterer doing weddings or large events should offer one. If they won't, that's a signal.
- **What's the cancellation policy?** Life happens. Know the window.

## Wedding catering tips

- Book 6-8 months out for peak season (June-September). You lose options fast.
- Get the per-head price in writing with a clear headcount deadline.
- Ask whether they provide rentals (plates, linens, glassware) or if you need a separate vendor.
- Request a tasting with at least 3-4 menu items. Judge the food cold too: buffet food sits.
- Confirm the service captain's name and get a direct contact for day-of.

## How much does catering cost in Edmonton?

Rough ranges for 2026:

- **Drop-off platters:** $12-$20 per person
- **Buffet service:** $25-$50 per person
- **Plated dinner service:** $45-$85 per person
- **Premium wedding catering:** $75-$150+ per person

These don't include rentals or staffing surcharges. Always get an itemized quote.
