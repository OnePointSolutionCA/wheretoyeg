Edmonton has turned into a wing town. The arrival of Wing Snob a couple of years ago lifted the whole scene, and local shops have raised their game. This is where to go when the craving hits.

## Best wings in Edmonton

**[Wing Snob Castle Downs](/restaurants/wing-snob-castle-downs):** 4.9 stars, 680 reviews. Castle Downs. The flagship for a lot of Edmonton wing fans. Nineteen sauces, dry rubs that actually taste like something, and the breading stays crispy even after a drive home. Honey garlic parmesan is the gateway. Mango habanero is the real test.

**[6IXTY WINGS](/restaurants/6ixty-wings):** 4.8 stars. Sherwood Park. Smaller operation, bigger flavours. Dry rubs are the move here. The lemon pepper and the Caribbean jerk are consistently great.

**[Wing Snob Sherwood Park](/restaurants/wing-snob-sherwood-park):** 4.6 stars. Sherwood Park. Same menu as Castle Downs. Ordering ahead for pickup is faster than most Friday nights at other chains.

**[Big Wings Edmonton](/restaurants/big-wings-edmonton):** 4.3 stars. 124 Street. Oversized wings, which is either a selling point or a problem depending on how you feel about ratios. Boneless and bone-in both solid. Good late night option in the core.

## Best wings for a crowd

Pre-ordering for a group is where Wing Snob pulls ahead. Fifty wings in two sauces, fries and a dip for about $70 feeds four. If you have mixed eaters, order a hot sauce and a sweet one so nobody loses.

## Halal wings in Edmonton

Several Edmonton wing spots are halal. Confirm with each location before you order, because franchises sometimes vary.

- Wing Snob locations are generally halal across the chain. Call your location to confirm.
- Many Pakistani and Middle Eastern restaurants in Edmonton do a strong wing. See the [Pakistani food guide](/blog/best-pakistani-food-edmonton-2026) for a few.

## Takeout vs. dine in

Wings are a takeout order. The dine in rooms at most Edmonton wing shops are small and more about pickup logistics than ambience. Order ahead by 20 minutes and skip the wait.
