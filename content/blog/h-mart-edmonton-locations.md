H Mart has two stores in Edmonton: **H Mart Edmonton South** at #570, 3803 Calgary Trail NW, and **H Mart Edmonton UA** at 8225 112 Street NW, near the University of Alberta. Both are open every day from 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM. H Mart is a Korean supermarket, and both stores sell Korean and other Asian groceries, fresh produce, snacks and hot food you can eat in the store.

The addresses, phone numbers and hours below come from H Mart Alberta's own website (ab.hmart.ca) and our listings, checked in November 2026. Hours can change on holidays, so call ahead on those days.

## H Mart Edmonton South (Calgary Trail)

The [Calgary Trail store](/grocery-markets/h-mart) is the more reviewed of the two, with more than 2,200 Google reviews and a 4.3 star rating. Shoppers in our listing's reviews point to:

- **Fresh seafood, fruit and vegetables.**
- **A food court** with Korean dishes at low prices, plus a bakery that one regular calls outstanding.
- **Korean snacks and pantry staples** for home cooking.
- **Things beyond food:** Korean kitchen tools, cosmetics and hair products that are hard to find elsewhere in Edmonton.
- **Wide aisles,** with sale items clearly labelled.

## H Mart Edmonton UA (112 Street)

The [112 Street store](/grocery-markets/h-mart-2) sits near the University of Alberta and has a 4.2 star rating from about 480 Google reviews. Reviewers say:

- It carries a big range of **Korean and Japanese products,** from ramen to Asian candy.
- It has **fresh produce** and **personal and household goods** as well as groceries.
- There is a **restaurant inside** the store.
- **Parking is very limited,** so most shoppers walk in.

## What should you buy at H Mart?

If it is your first visit, start with the things shoppers mention most: fresh fruit and vegetables, the seafood counter, Korean snacks and ramen, and a hot meal or bakery item from the food court. Look for the labelled sale items as you go. If you cook Korean food at home, it is also the place for Korean kitchen tools.

## When is H Mart open?

Both Edmonton stores are open seven days a week, 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM, on H Mart Alberta's website and on our listings. On holidays like Christmas Day, New Year's Day or Lunar New Year, call the store first.
