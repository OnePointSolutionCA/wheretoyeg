Edmonton's Lebanese food scene punches way above its weight. From the shawarma spots on 118 Ave to the sit-down restaurants on Whyte, there's great food in every corner of the city. These are the ones with the ratings and the lineups to back it up.

## Best Lebanese restaurants in Edmonton

**[Turquaz Kebab House](/restaurants/turquaz-kebab-house):** 4.7 stars, 9,212 reviews. Edmonton. Technically Turkish-Mediterranean, but the shawarma and grilled platters are among the best in the city. The volume of reviews tells you how consistent they are.

**[Fattoush](/restaurants/fattoush):** 5.0 stars, 497 reviews. Whyte Ave. The highest rated Mediterranean restaurant on Whyte. Sit down, order the shareable mezze platter, add the kafta. Good for date night.

**[Root of Beirut](/restaurants/root-of-beirut):** 4.4 stars, 1,344 reviews. North Edmonton. Classic Lebanese comfort food. The shawarma plate is huge. The garlic sauce is the real reason regulars come back.

**[Paramount Fine Foods](/restaurants/paramount-fine-foods):** 4.4 stars, 1,547 reviews. North Edmonton. Full menu Lebanese chain with a strong Edmonton location. Good for groups and larger orders.

**[Master Pita](/restaurants/master-pita):** 4.9 stars, 760 reviews. St. Albert. Fresh pita baked all day. The shawarma and the manakeesh are the signature orders.

## Best shawarma in Edmonton

**[Jerusalem Shawarma Downtown](/restaurants/jerusalem-shawarma-downtown):** 4.9 stars, 5,649 reviews. One of the all-time Edmonton shawarma shops. Chicken shawarma plate with extra garlic sauce is the move.

**[Tahini's Shawarma](/restaurants/tahinis-shawarma-2):** 4.8 stars. Edmonton. Fresh ingredients, bigger portions than most. The Family Feast is a good shortcut for groups.

**[Shawarma & Mediterranean Flavors](/restaurants/shawarma-mediterranean-flavors):** 4.9 stars, 297 reviews. Edmonton. Smaller shop with a steady following. Honest prices, generous portions.

For more, see the [best shawarma in Edmonton guide](/blog/best-shawarma-edmonton-2026).

## Best Mediterranean mezze and sit down

**[Zaatar w Zeit](/restaurants/zaatar-w-zeit):** 4.6 stars, 2,057 reviews. West Edmonton. Great for weekend brunch. Manakeesh with zaatar, labneh, fattoush salad. Bring friends.

**[Donair & Shawarma House Sherwood Park](/restaurants/donair-shawarma-house-sherwood-park):** 4.6 stars, 1,491 reviews. Sherwood Park. Big menu, fast service, solid prices.

**[Basha Donair & Shawarma](/restaurants/basha-donair-shawarma-south-edmonton):** 4.4 stars. South Edmonton. A local chain that has been around long enough that most Edmontonians have an opinion. The donairs are the real draw.

## Best pita bakery

**[Alhuda Pita Bakery & Restaurant](/bakeries/alhuda-pita-bakery-restaurant):** 4.5 stars, 352 reviews. North Edmonton. Fresh pita baked every day with no preservatives. They supply local restaurants and stores, and the attached restaurant does strong manakeesh, shawarma and grilled platters.

**[Sunbake Pita Bakery](/bakeries/sunbake-pita-bakery):** 4.6 stars, 2,195 reviews. North Edmonton. Edmonton staple for fresh pita. Pick up a bag on your way home. Grab Lebanese pastries while you're there.

## Halal and dietary

Most Lebanese restaurants in Edmonton are halal. Confirm with each spot, especially the Turkish ones that sometimes serve a mixed menu. Vegetarians have plenty of options at every restaurant on this list, from fattoush to mujadara to the full mezze spread.
