## How to choose

- **Get a quote based on a walkthrough, not square footage alone.** A cluttered 1,200 sq ft condo takes longer than a tidy 1,800 sq ft house.
- **Ask if they bring their own supplies.** Most professional services do. If they use your vacuum and your Windex, negotiate the price down.
- **Check insurance.** If a cleaner breaks something or gets injured in your home, their insurance covers it. No insurance means you're liable.
- **Read the cancellation policy.** Most companies charge $50–100 for cancellations within 24 hours.
