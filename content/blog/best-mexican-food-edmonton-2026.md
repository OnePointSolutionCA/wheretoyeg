Edmonton's Mexican food scene has quietly become one of the best on the prairies. Not the chain kind. Small taquerias where the carne asada has real char, owner-run spots where the regulars get greeted by name, and birria joints that hand you a cup of consommé for dipping.

## What makes good Mexican food good

- **The tortilla matters.** Corn for street tacos, warm and slightly charred. If a place presses its own, the rest of the menu is usually dialed in too.
- **The salsa.** A serious Mexican spot has more than one: a red, a green, and something with heat.
- **Protein done right.** Carne asada should be grilled hard and chopped fine. Birria should have depth, not just chili powder. Pollo asado should be juicy, not dry.
- **Portions match the price.** Street tacos should run $3 to $5 each. At $8 a taco, the spot better be doing something special.

## Avoiding pork?

A lot of classic Mexican dishes are pork-based (al pastor, carnitas, chorizo), and many taquerias cook every protein on the same flat-top. If you avoid pork, ask two things before ordering: which meats are pork, and whether the beef and chicken are cooked on a separate grill. Spots that take the question seriously usually have a clear answer.

## Beef and chicken tacos worth knowing

- **Carne asada:** Grilled marinated beef, chopped and served with onion and cilantro. The benchmark.
- **Birria:** Beef stewed in a rich chili broth. Served with consommé for dipping.
- **Barbacoa:** Slow-cooked beef cheek. Rich and fatty, and it melts.
- **Suadero:** Thin-cut beef, crisped on the edges.
- **Lengua:** Beef tongue. Sounds intimidating, but done right it's tender and buttery.
- **Pollo asado:** Grilled marinated chicken. The safe pick that's still good.

## How much should tacos cost in Edmonton?

- **Street-style tacos:** $3 to $5 each
- **Sit-down restaurant tacos:** $5 to $8 each
- **Burrito plates:** $14 to $18
- **Full sit-down dinner:** $20 to $35 per person

If a casual counter spot works out to more than $5 a taco, you're paying for the room, not the food.
