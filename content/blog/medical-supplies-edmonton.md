Whether you're recovering from surgery, caring for a parent, or living with a chronic condition, getting the right medical supplies matters. Here's where Edmonton locals shop.

## Locally owned specialists

**[1st AB Med Supplies](/medical/medical-supplies)** has four locations across the city — 13514 97 St NW, 15020 116 Ave NW, 5809 118 Ave NW, and 4245 118 Ave NW. Each store carries the full range: mobility aids (walkers, canes, wheelchairs), wound care, incontinence supplies, home health equipment, and daily-living products. All four offer in-store shopping, curbside pickup, and delivery.

**Med Supplies Edmonton** at 10134 111 Ave NW is another trusted local option — 4.8 stars over 200+ reviews, and known for patient, knowledgeable staff. In-store shopping, curbside, and no-contact delivery.

## What insurance usually covers

If you have extended health benefits (Blue Cross, Sun Life, Manulife, etc.), most of these are commonly covered — get a prescription from your doctor and submit for reimbursement:

- Compression stockings
- CPAP masks and supplies
- Diabetic supplies (glucose meters, test strips)
- Ostomy supplies
- Wound-care dressings after surgery
- Mobility equipment (with a prescription)

## Tips before you buy

- **Get measured.** Walkers, canes, and wheelchairs are height-specific. Both 1st AB and Med Supplies will fit you in-store — don't guess online.
- **Ask about used or rental options** for short-term needs like post-surgery recovery.
- **Bring your prescription** — insurance usually needs it.
