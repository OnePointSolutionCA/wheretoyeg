Half of picking a barber is knowing what to look for before you sit down. A quick guide from the WhereToYEG team.

## Look at their portfolio, not the shop

A clean, curated Instagram beats a fancy interior every time. If a barber posts their own work regularly — and the fades look sharp on the last swipe — that's a green light.

## Ask about the clipper work

If you want a skin fade or a low taper, the finish is everything. Ask how long they typically spend on a fade. Under 20 minutes and you're getting rushed.

## Check the reviews for consistency

One or two glowing reviews mean little. **Twenty consistent 5-stars over six months** is what you want. On [our barbers page](/barbers), you can sort by rating and review count.

## Book, don't walk in (the first time)

Even at walk-in-friendly shops, book your first cut. It gives the barber time to actually understand what you want.
