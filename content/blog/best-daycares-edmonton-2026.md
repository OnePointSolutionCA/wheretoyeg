Finding a daycare in Edmonton is hard. Spots fill up months ahead, prices are all over the map, and every centre's website says the same thing about "nurturing, play-based learning". The reviews on Google are what separate the ones families stay at from the ones they leave after a month. These are the Edmonton childcare centres with the highest ratings and real review counts.

## What to actually check before you enroll

Reviews only tell you so much. When you tour, pay attention to:

- How staff talk to the kids who aren't being watched by parents. Easy to spot.
- Staff turnover. Ask how long the room educators have been there.
- Real daily reports vs. "She had a great day." Centres that log naps, meals and activities give you a window into the day.
- Outdoor space. Edmonton kids are outside a lot. Fenced yard, equipment, shade matter.
- Nap room setup and food prep area.
- The waitlist process. If they say "we have a spot right now," ask why.
