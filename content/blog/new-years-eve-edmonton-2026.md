For a family New Year's Eve in Edmonton in 2026, the surest bet is Strathcona County's free New Year's Festival in Sherwood Park on Thursday, December 31, from 6 to 8 PM, with fireworks over Broadmoor Lake Park at 8 PM. For a warm indoor night, World Waterpark at West Edmonton Mall holds its New Year's Eve Beach Ball from 6 PM to midnight.

The City of Edmonton's downtown festival and fireworks at Churchill Square is on the City's website, but its 2026 times were not posted yet when we checked.

## What is happening at the Sherwood Park New Year's Festival?

Strathcona County's [New Year's Festival](https://strathcona.ca/recreation-events/festivals-and-events/new-years-eve-festival) runs from 6 to 8 PM on December 31, 2026, and the county describes it as free and family friendly. Donations for the Strathcona Food Bank are collected at the festival. Activities run from 6 to 7:50 PM in four spots close together:

- **Festival Place:** a LEGO display, glow stick handouts and aerial artist shows at 6, 6:45 and 7:30 PM.
- **Sherwood Park Arena and Sports Centre:** free recreational skating (bring your own skates, helmets strongly recommended), a Sherwood Park Skating Club performance from 6:50 PM, a hockey shootout and the Lost Child Station.
- **Peace Park and the Festival Place parking lot:** snowshoeing and s'mores.
- **Broadmoor Lake Park Plaza:** holiday games, ice sculptures, a cabane à sucre, a warming zone and fire dancer shows at 6:15 and 7:15 PM.

The fireworks start at **8 PM over Broadmoor Lake Park**. If you watch from your car, tune to 107.5 FM for the music that goes with the show. An accessible amenities map on the county's page shows earplugs for sensory needs, first aid, washrooms and accessible routes.

**Getting there:** Strathcona County Transit runs a free Park 'N Ride from Bethel Transit Terminal (650 Bethel Drive) starting at 5:30 PM, every 15 minutes, with the last bus back leaving Festival Place at 8:45 PM.

The 8 PM fireworks make this the easiest one for younger kids who will not make it to midnight.

## What is the New Year's Eve Beach Ball at World Waterpark?

[World Waterpark](/activities-fun/world-waterpark) at West Edmonton Mall (8882 170 St NW) holds its [New Year's Eve Beach Ball](https://www.wem.ca/events/new-years-eve-beach-ball-2027/) on December 31 from 6 PM to midnight. WEM's page promises 31°C temperatures, a live DJ, live performances and an indoor fireworks finale at the midnight countdown. Ticket prices for 2026 were not on the page when we checked, so look there before you go.

## Are there New Year's Eve fireworks in downtown Edmonton?

Yes, the City of Edmonton's website invites families to its [New Year's Eve Downtown Festival and Fireworks](https://www.edmonton.ca/attractions_events/schedule_festivals_events/new-years-eve) at Sir Winston Churchill Square. As of November 9, 2026, the City's page did not list the 2026 schedule or fireworks times yet. We will update this guide when the City posts them.

## Does St. Albert have New Year's Eve fireworks?

St. Albert held free [New Year's Eve fireworks](https://stalbert.ca/events/calendar/community/new-years-eve-fireworks/) on December 31, 2025, starting at 8:30 PM from the Meadowview Ball Diamonds. That site itself is closed to the public, and the City suggested watching from spots like Lions Park and Millennium Park. The 2026 show was not posted yet when we checked.
