Edmonton's halal restaurant scene has quietly become one of the strongest in western Canada. Here's a working shortlist of spots worth planning your week around.

## Late-night halal on Jasper Ave

Downtown's north-of-Jasper corridor stays open when the rest of the city sleeps. Shawarma wraps, garlic sauce, and honest prices — three things Edmonton delivers on hard. Start with **[Sam's Shawarma](/halal-restaurants/sams-shawarma)** and work outwards.

## South Edmonton biryani

Along Calgary Trail and out toward Ellerslie, halal Pakistani and Indian kitchens have been quietly opening for years. If you know one worth listing, [tell us](/contact) and we'll add it.

## Halal brunch

An underrated category. Rolled omelettes, halal breakfast sausages, saffron French toast — the pockets around Mill Woods and West Edmonton have this on lock.

## What to look for

- Certification displayed at the counter
- Chef or owner on-site
- Menu that hasn't sprawled — five great things beat fifteen decent ones
