Edmonton winter is long. Pretending otherwise is how people end up losing their minds in January. The city has more to do in the cold than most people realize, from skating under lights to indoor climbing walls to museums you've probably never been to. Here's where to actually go.

## Best outdoor skating in Edmonton

Our outdoor rinks are a big part of winter. The maintained ones stay beautiful most of the season.

**[Victoria Park IceWay](/activities-fun/victoria-iceway-skating-trail):** 4.6 stars. Edmonton. Long maintained skating trail along the river valley. Wind through the trees. Open evenings with lights.

**[Hawrelak Park Pond Ice](/activities-fun/hawrelak-park-pond-ice):** 4.6 stars, 1,051 reviews. Edmonton. The classic family skate. Big pond, bonfire pits, warm up hut.

**[Rundle Park Outdoor Skating IceWay](/activities-fun/rundle-park-outdoor-skating-iceway):** 4.6 stars. Beverly. Skating path through the park. Less busy than Victoria, free to use.

**[Victoria Park Skating Oval](/activities-fun/victoria-park-skating-oval):** 4.7 stars. Edmonton. If you're serious about long blade speed skating, this is where to go.

For indoor skating when it's too cold even for Edmontonians, see the full list of [skating in Edmonton](/activities-fun/skating).

## Museums and warm indoor stops

**[TELUS World of Science Edmonton](/activities-fun/telus-world-of-science):** 4.5 stars, 2,324 reviews. West Edmonton. The go-to with kids. The Science Garage and the IMAX fill a full day.

**[Royal Alberta Museum](/activities-fun/royal-alberta-museum):** 4.6 stars, 5,039 reviews. Downtown. Massive. Natural history on main, culture upstairs. Easy half day.

**[Muttart Conservatory](/activities-fun/muttart-conservatory):** 4.7 stars, 2,548 reviews. Central Edmonton. The warm pyramids in the middle of a freezing day are one of the best ideas Edmonton ever had.

**[Art Gallery of Alberta](/activities-fun/art-gallery-of-alberta):** 4.3 stars. Downtown. Rotating shows, built-in cafe. Good quiet afternoon.

**[Museum of Illusions](/activities-fun/museum-of-illusions):** 4.7 stars. West Edmonton. More fun with kids and teens than you'd expect.

**[Neon Sign Museum](/activities-fun/neon-sign-museum):** 4.5 stars. Downtown. Free, outdoor, surprisingly fun to walk past at night.

## Best indoor playgrounds for kids

When your kid needs to burn energy and it's -25:

**[Bouncy Planet](/activities-fun/bouncy-planet):** 4.9 stars, 2,323 reviews. Edmonton. Clean, well staffed. Great for ages 2 to 8.

**[Fun4all Indoor Playground](/activities-fun/fun4all-indoor-playground):** 4.8 stars, 1,432 reviews. Edmonton. Big play structure, cafe for parents.

**[U-Play Indoor Playground](/activities-fun/u-play-indoor-playground-in-edmonton):** 4.5 stars, 3,193 reviews. Edmonton. Longstanding Edmonton favourite with huge play area.

**[LaunchPad Trampoline Park (184 St)](/activities-fun/launchpad-trampoline-park-2):** 4.7 stars, 1,469 reviews. Older kids and teens burn hours here.

Browse every [indoor playground](/activities-fun/indoor-playground) and every [trampoline park](/activities-fun/trampoline).

## Fort Edmonton Park and the Botanic Garden

**[Fort Edmonton Park](/activities-fun/fort-edmonton-park):** 4.5 stars, 5,378 reviews. Winter hours are limited, but the Dark Fort and Christmas events are worth planning around.

**[U of A Botanic Garden](/activities-fun/u-of-a-botanic-garden):** 4.6 stars, 3,320 reviews. The Luminaria event in December is one of Edmonton's best winter traditions. Book early.
