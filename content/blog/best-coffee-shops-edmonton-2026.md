Edmonton has quietly built a serious coffee scene. There's the Tim Hortons crowd, and then there's everyone else — locally roasted beans, actual latte art, and cafes that treat espresso like the craft it is. This guide is about the second group.

## What to look for

- **Freshly roasted beans.** Roast date should be within the last 3–4 weeks. Ask.
- **A properly dialed espresso machine.** The shot should pull in 25–32 seconds. Faster = under-extracted (sour). Slower = over-extracted (bitter).
- **Real steamed milk.** Silky microfoam, not stiff bubbles. Latte art is a byproduct of good milk texture, which is why it matters.
- **A menu that isn't 40 items long.** Focused shops make better coffee.

## Top coffee shops in Edmonton

**[Credo Coffee](/cafes-coffee-shops/credo-coffee)** — Multi-location Edmonton staple. Consistent espresso, quiet atmospheres to work in, and one of the best places in the city to learn what good coffee tastes like.

**[Rogue Wave Coffee](/cafes-coffee-shops/rogue-wave-coffee)** — Small-batch roaster, minimalist rooms, serious about origin. If you care about the difference between Ethiopian and Colombian beans, this is your shop.

**[Dream Tea House](/cafes-coffee-shops/dream-tea-house)** — More tea than coffee, but the best boba in the city and a solid spot to sit for a couple hours.

**[Presotea](/cafes-coffee-shops/presotea-edmonton)** — Taiwanese-style fresh-brewed tea, great alternative when you need caffeine but not another latte.

**[Tiger Sugar](/cafes-coffee-shops/tiger-sugar)** — Brown-sugar boba, Insta-famous striped drinks, worth trying at least once.

**[Kind Ice Cream](/cafes-coffee-shops/kind-ice-cream)** — Not coffee — but affogatos here are a legitimate afternoon coffee move. Coral-berry sorbet with a shot of espresso, thank me later.

## Best coffee shops for working remotely

If you're looking for wifi + a table you can occupy for 2–3 hours without side-eye:

- **Weekdays before 11am** — most shops are quiet and welcome laptops.
- **Avoid weekend mornings** — brunch traffic makes anyone with a laptop look like the enemy.
- **Buy something every hour.** It's the deal.
- **Bring headphones.** Ambient noise is fine; being the guy on speakerphone is not.

## Best coffee near you, by neighborhood

- **Downtown (Jasper Ave, 104 St)** — Credo has locations here; most walkable coffee stretch in the city.
- **124 Street** — Independent-heavy, brunch-friendly, third-wave-forward.
- **Whyte Ave (Old Strathcona)** — Rogue Wave and neighborhood shops with real character.
- **South Edmonton** — Coffee is more scattered here but there are gems worth the drive.
- **North Edmonton** — Neighborhood shops with strong regulars.

## What to order

- **Espresso** — the truth serum. If it tastes bad here, everything else will too.
- **Flat white** — the barista's drink. Cortado-level ratio, silky texture, no drowning the coffee.
- **Latte** — more milk, more forgiving. Order this if you like your coffee milky.
- **Pour-over / drip** — best way to actually taste a specific single-origin bean. Ask what's freshly roasted.
- **Cold brew** — smooth, less acidic, higher caffeine. Different beast from iced coffee.
