Whyte Ave (Old Strathcona) is Edmonton's most consistent neighborhood. Independent shops, restaurants that stay honest, a walkability the rest of the city envies. Here's how to spend a Saturday.

## Morning: coffee first

Start with a proper espresso at **[Third Wave Coffee Bar](/cafes-coffee-shops/third-wave-coffee-bar)** on 82 Ave. Grab a pastry, sit at the window, and let the ave wake up around you.

## Midday: a real haircut

Whyte has more good barbers per block than any other Edmonton neighborhood. **[Fades by Mike](/barbers/fades-by-mike)** is worth the wait on weekends.

## Afternoon: browse

Independent bookstores, vintage clothing, records, and thrift. If you have $40 in your pocket you'll leave with something you'll actually use.

## Evening: eat + drink

Halal, Vietnamese, Japanese, Ethiopian, Italian — Whyte's kitchens punch above their weight. Filter by cuisine on [restaurants](/restaurants) or [halal-restaurants](/halal-restaurants).

## Late night

Shawarma, poutine, pizza slices — that's the trilogy. All open past 1 AM on weekends.
