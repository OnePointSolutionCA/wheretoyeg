      I booked an appointment for a check engine light on my car and dropped it off late on a Friday afternoon. They ran a 5 minute diagnostic while I was there and quoted me around $210 for the repair. I thought that was reasonable and left the car with them.
      
      When I picked it up the next day, the bill was $300. When I asked about the difference, I was told it was a diagnostic charge. At no point, from booking the appointment to dropping off the car, was a diagnostic fee mentioned, including when they gave me the $210 quote right after running the diagnostic.
      
      I have no problem paying for a diagnostic. I understand I’m paying for expertise and equipment, plus the time. My problem is that it wasn’t disclosed. A fee like that should be stated when the appointment is booked, or at the very least included in the quote, not added at pickup.
      
      After arguing and them refusing to budge, I paid the bill and left. On the drive home, I noticed a rattle that wasn’t there before I dropped the car off. I pulled over and checked. I went back to the shop and explained the noise, and was told there was no issue and that it was normal.
      
      I’ve since booked an appointment at another shop to diagnose the rattle, one that told me their diagnostic fee upfront when I booked.
