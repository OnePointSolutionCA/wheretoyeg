      We have been going back to Big Foot pizza a few times since this order. The base sauce is delicious, the crust is soft where it needs to be and sturdy at the ends for an easy hand hold. The first time around, we ordered the butter chicken and the pepperoni personal sized pizzas. One of the ladies here recommended the garlic dip (which are all complimentary) with the butter chicken - and I can confirm that was a good recommendation. The butter chicken pizza itself is quite good, the chicken is meaty and juicy indicating a good cut of chicken, the sauce and seasoning was exactly butter chicken as you'd expect from a regular spot. The pepperoni was the winner for us though - the cheese had a good pull, great chew, and the pepperoni was crispy and cupped, without being too greasy. The marinara sauce served with it was fresh, a little tangy and flavourful. We have since returned for several large pizzas, mostly the pepperoni with extra mozzarella and hawaiian. The only qualm with this place is how warm the restaurant gets, but it's understandable considering the close quarters and open kitchen design. The staff here have been kind and I love that their boxes have handwritten messages. I can't believe I've been driving past this place for so long - and yet, that's probably a good thing since I will be attributing my recent weight gain to my frequent patronage of this establishment. Can't wait to go back for more!
