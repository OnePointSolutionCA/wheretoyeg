      It was a good experience, but I don't think I'd go back again.
      
      Luckily, we arrived early and were seated right away. The all-you-can-eat selection of raw meats and seafood was definitely a highlight. There were a few vegetables available, but there was a much larger selection of pre-cooked dishes.
      
      For dessert, they had small pastries, grapes, Jell-O cups, and Melona popsicles.
      
      Personally, I think this place is a bit overhyped. For the price, I expected a larger variety and quantity of food. While the raw meats and seafood were nice, the overall selection felt limited, and I didn't feel it offered enough value for what we paid.
      
      The atmosphere was nice, especially with all the Marvel-themed figurines throughout the restaurant. One thing I really appreciated was how attentive the staff were—they came by to replace the grill about every 15 minutes, which kept everything clean and made cooking much more enjoyable.
      
      Overall, it was a fun experience to try once, but I don't think it's somewhere I'd return.
