    comment: |
      Interior: Top-notch—modern, stylish, and very inviting. The location is easy to find, and since we went early on a Sunday, there was plenty of street parking available.
      
      Service was great, likely because we were the only customers at the time. One thing to note for families: they don’t have high chairs or booster seats, so keep that in mind if you’re bringing little ones.
      
      As for the food, it was average overall.
      
      * Korean Chicken Burger: The chicken was crispy, juicy, and flavorful. If you enjoy a saucy burger, this is a solid choice.
      * Chili Maple Glazed Chicken: Nicely cooked with a pleasant balance of sweet and spicy flavors, but it didn’t leave a lasting impression.
      * Scallops: Overpriced for just three small pieces, and the flavor wasn’t anything spectacular.
      * Steak and Arugula Salad: Fresh ingredients, but overall quite average and nothing particularly memorable.
      
      Overall, it’s a beautiful restaurant with a convenient location and good service, but the food didn’t quite match the quality of the ambiance.
