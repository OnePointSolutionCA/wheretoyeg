      Warm, flavourful Indian food with rich sauces, tender meat, fresh garlic naan, and excellent staff recommendations — the only regret was not arriving hungrier.
      
      For a fuller picture —
      Zaika of India in Beaumont is a lovely spot for a warm, satisfying Indian meal. On this visit, we had the chicken lababdar, Kadai lamb, garlic naan, and plain rice, and everything came together beautifully.
      
      The staff made excellent recommendations, and they were absolutely worth following. The chicken lababdar was rich, creamy, and comforting, with that deep, rounded flavour that keeps you going back for the next bite. The Kadai lamb brought a hearty depth, with tender meat and a nice balance of spice. Paired with the garlic naan and plain rice, it was exactly the kind of meal that feels easy to share, easy to enjoy, and very worth returning for.
      
      Our only regret was that we were not able to try more food, buy more to take away, or arrive hungrier so we could order more dishes. That is usually the best sign of a good restaurant — when you are already planning what to try next before the meal is even over.
      
      Flavourful, aromatic, generous, and welcoming. Definitely a great Beaumont spot to return to and explore more of the menu.
