      I was craving Chinese food for a little while. Me and my bf were in the city and stumbled across this place while searching somewhere to eat for dinner. We trusted the good reviews and decided to try it out.
      
      We got the two person dinner and selected lemon chicken and beef chowmein for our two additional sides. Everything was fresh and full of flavour. However the beef chowmein lacked something in taste and isn’t what I expected. It had a sauce similar to beef and broccoli.
      
      One minor thing when dining in—is that the waitresses are a bit slow. We got seated and waited maybe 10-12mins to place our order. Food came around the 20 min mark after the waitress took our order.
      
      Overall though, a great place for Chinese food. We’ll be back again soon to try more.
