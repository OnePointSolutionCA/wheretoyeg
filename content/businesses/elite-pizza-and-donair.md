      I recently visited this gem of a spot with Shad hunter to take their food challenge. What truly stood out was the heart and soul behind the place: the owners, a beautiful couple whose love and commitment radiates not just in their relationship but in every bite of their food.
      
      I had the pleasure of chatting with the lovely woman who co-owns the restaurant — she was an absolute angel. Warm, genuine, and passionate about what they do. You can feel that care in their service, and especially in their food.

That means you're getting a richer, fuller flavor that speaks for itself. It’s a small detail, but it makes a huge difference, and it shows that they really care about what they’re serving.
