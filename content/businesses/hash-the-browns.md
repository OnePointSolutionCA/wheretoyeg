      We have found a new first stop breakfast place in Hash The Browns, it's a bit of a drive out to it from where we live but it's worth it. The food is excellent, a very large menu to pick and choose from, and the atmosphere is nice, never too loud for the time of day we go.
      
      I do wish, and this is pretty personal, that they offered some more meat alternatives like turkey bacon on their menu versus the many variations of the  same food items, but that's a very nitpicky thing
      
      I also wish their coffee was a bit better, it's everything you kind of expect from a breakfast place but I feel like maybe they need to take a look at the coffee brewing process to see if there's anything needing fixing just because it tastes quite burnt.
      
      All in all, I highly recommend the place, especially if you are celiac and want to feel safe eating there without fear of being glutened!

      My family and I visited here on a Saturday at around 11 in the morning, we were seated immediately, the staff was attentive and friendly; happily engaging with my youngest's antics.
      
      We ordered our meals and drinks and hardly had a chance to stir the cream and sugar into the coffee before our full meals had been cooked and delivered to the table.
      I had the Hungryman Breakfast combo with a side of fresh fruit. The meal was perfectly portioned, and well plated. I specifically loved the bacon, as it was perfectly done, not too soft and not a piece of cardboard.
      My girlfriend had the Denver Benny and states that it was very good, specifically mentioning the holliandaise was properly flavored and the poached eggs perfectly done.
      The youngest had the Little Flipper which is a crepe with bananas, strawberries and chocolate sauce and it was the perfect portion size for them.
      
      They lost a star in Atmosphere only for the lack of decor and the very retro style furniture, the TV's also were playing sports, otherwise it was a very comfortable and clean location.
      
      I will 100% be returning for more meals in the future.
