      I was ecstatic to try this bakery.
      Ordered breakfast for dine in.
      We had baker's breakfast and Ryan's (grilled cheese with smoked salmon).
      The server was friendly.
      I asked for a knife. My order was was a garlic sourdough toast with eggs and bacon on it. They didn't have a knife but was able to lend me a paring knife.
      
      Pro tip: if you are planning on dining in, it is important to know that they do not serve coffee or any drinks in the menu. They have a water dispenser which you can ask for a cup from the server.
      There is a Cafe upstairs where I bought my latte.
      
      Price was decent 👌 for the quality of the food.
      We bought a loaf of sourdough and 3 kinds of croissant to go.
      
      Expectation vs Reality
      Ordered for a passion fruit croissant. On their Instagram post you can see how filled their croissant is with its filling. I cut mine in the middle and you can barely see the filling. The filling was just on top of my croissant and was very tiny. So this was a disappointment for me.
      
      Sourdough loaf, chocolate croissant and coffee espresso croissant were great.
      
      I like the ambiance of the store.
      
      Would still recommend this store as long as you make a note of other stuff in this review.
