      A great restaurant in the Garneau/UofA area.  It has a real nice neighbourhood feel with lots of regulars.  They have a nice patio for the summer months.  Great coffee and a large freezer display case of all the different house made gelato's that they have.

I have a bad habit of digging into the food without taking a review photo first so for the pizza all you see is the empty pan and my last bite of pizza.  Normally we don't have desert however when I heard the bartender say the word "Crumble" I knew we had to investigate the desert menu.  It was the Crumble di Mele with fire roasted Granny Smith apples, cinnamon, caramel, oats, pecans, and walnuts topped with the gelato of ones choice (we had the vanilla).  It was absolutely delicious!  We enjoyed the desert with snifters of Montenegro Amaro which was a nice way to finish a great meal and a great time.
