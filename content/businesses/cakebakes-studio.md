      I ordered a cake for my son's birthday, and I am extremely disappointed with what I received. I paid $120 for a cake that turned out to be more icing than actual cake.
      
      First, I specifically ordered a red velvet and vanilla cake. Naturally, I expected two cake flavours, red velvet and vanilla. However, when we cut the cake, it was entirely red velvet. When I complained to the baker, she told me that the vanilla was the icing. How does that make sense? If a customer orders a red velvet and vanilla cake, wouldn't you expect the cake itself to contain both flavours? If the vanilla was only going to be the icing, that should have been clearly communicated before I placed my order.
      
      But that wasn't even the most disappointing part. More than 50% of this cake was icing! There were thin layers of actual cake separated by ridiculously thick layers of icing. As you can see in the picture, the amount of icing compared to the cake itself was unbelievable. I ordered a cake, not a bucket of icing. If I wanted to eat icing, I would have ordered icing!
      
      When I raised this concern with the baker, she told me I should have specified that I wanted thick layers of cake. Seriously? Since when does a customer have to explain to a baker that they want more cake than icing when ordering a cake? Isn't the cake supposed to be the main product?
      
      She also insisted that this was the standard. Well, I have bought and eaten cakes from different bakeries, and I have never had to specifically request that the actual cake should make up a reasonable portion of what I am paying for.
      
      What makes this even more frustrating is that I paid $120 for this cake. I could have walked into Walmart, bought a cake for around $30, and probably gotten something bigger with a much better cake-to-icing ratio.
      
      This was supposed to be a special cake for my son's birthday, and instead, I feel like I wasted my money. It is not just about the price. It is about receiving what you ordered and getting value for your money.
      
      I would not recommend this bakery based on my experience. If you enjoy eating large amounts of icing with a little cake in between, perhaps you will be satisfied. But if you actually want to enjoy a proper cake, I would suggest looking elsewhere.
      
      Very disappointing experience, and certainly not worth the $120 I paid.
