      If you’re not drooling yet… A Northside location for Seoul Fried Chicken! Heck yeah! Whenever we were near Whyte ave this place is always on our mind but now with an excellent location on 137Ave right near TnT I know we will be hitting this up more often! The parking here can get busy but there is usually a spot. This is definitely more of a take home and enjoy kind of place and style anyways.
      The chicken is so juicy and tender, the flavours rock your socks and thOur last visit we ordered several chicken boxes so we could try most of their flavors: 4 types of cuts of chicken. Drumstick, Wing, Tender Piece, and Bone-in Thigh. OR All Tender offerings are also available. Tossed with any flavour of your choice or Sauce on the Side. Sauce wise we highly recommend the Garlic Soy, Golden Kari, Gochujang Caramel,Gochujang Garlic Soy, and the Sweet BBQ. Most of these are heat tossed and something about how the sauce sticks to that buttermilk flour egg battered and fried golden crispy coating makes them divine.
      Sides wise we enjoyed the salads- both the Kale Caesar Salad ($8) and the Black Sesame Slaw ($7) were delightful. We also quite liked the Sweet Butter Cukes ($4/$6).
