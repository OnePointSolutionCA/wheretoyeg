      Four of us had a really nice dinner at Mikado downtown tonight, in a comfortable booth with great service and delicious food.   Our Japanese friends ordered a variety of dishes ( including assorted tempura that I forget to take a photo of) and everything was very fresh and each with its own unique taste.
