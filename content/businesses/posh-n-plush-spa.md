      I booked a teeth whitening session here using a Groupon deal. When I arrived, the place was pretty quiet—only staff at the front desk, no other clients in sight. After completing the paperwork, the technician honored the Groupon discount in person, which was appreciated (since Groupon takes their cut).
      
      The actual treatment felt quite rushed. There was very little explanation or guidance—just quick instructions like "do this, do that," and then it was over before I knew it. The process itself (blue light and gel application) was straightforward, but the whole visit lacked any real personal touch or educational component about aftercare or expectations.
      
      Overall, it was an okay experience for the price, but the minimal customer service and in-and-out vibe left me feeling underwhelmed. I wouldn't make the drive from downtown to Beaumont again just for this. For similar results, I'd probably opt for an at-home LED whitening kit instead—half the value of these services comes from good customer interaction, and this one fell short there.
      
      If you're considering it purely for convenience and the discount, it might work, but don't expect a premium, attentive spa-like experience.
