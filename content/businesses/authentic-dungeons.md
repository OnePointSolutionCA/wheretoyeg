      Simply put, it is the best place in Edmonton to play Dungeons & Dragons and fill all your miniature based hobby game needs including but not limited to: Warhammer 40,000, Warhammer Age of Sigmar, Middle-Earth Battle Strategy Game, and of course Dungeons & Dragons.
      
      You can get the paints and other paraphernalia to make your miniatures match your exact specifications. You can even hire one of their experts to paint your miniatures for you. They also offer 3D printing services and brilliant advice on all your hobby gaming needs.
      
      They have rooms that are aesthetically detailed to enhance immersive play, which can be affordably booked to play your own games in. You can hire a GM to if you prefer; all of the GMs (Storytellers) do a remarkable job of making your experience thrilling, fun, and rememberable. In booking one of the rooms, you gain access to their expansive miniatures library, terrain, and game books. They also have snacks and drinks (including alcohol).
      
      They have themed nights in the store space, for example: Wednesday is paint night and Friday is Magic: The Gathering.
      
      This place is nerd heaven! (I say that as a proud nerd myself). The owners are kind and welcoming, and actually care for the people who walk through their doors!
