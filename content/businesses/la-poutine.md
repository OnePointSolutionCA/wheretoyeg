      Decent poutine for Alberta. Cheese curds are good. At first glance, looks like lots of cheese on the poutine, however, when you mix it up and start eating, it's minimal quantity. When you are used to Québec poutine which over-flows with cheese without asking for extra, this will seem light on cheese. The tradition 'Québec' gravy does not taste like gravy from Québec, it's rather very salty. The fries are okay. Overall for an Alberta poutine, it's acceptable. Of course, it's not comparable to a Québec poutine. Staff was very friendly. No bathroom, which seems odd for a restaurant.
