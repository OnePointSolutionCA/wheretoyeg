By the time our meals arrived, most of our guests were nearly finished, which made the dinner feel awkward and took away from the shared experience we had hoped for.

To their credit, they did not charge the cake service fee at the end, which we appreciated. However, the main concern was not the fee itself; it was the lack of transparency upfront.

      Original Review:
      The space had a warm and inviting feel. The carpeted bench seating gave the restaurant a cozy atmosphere, and a self-serve tea station was such a lovely touch. As big fans of Turkish cuisine, we always enjoy trying Turkish restaurants when we travel, so we were excited to try this spot.
      
      For lunch, we ordered the Iskender kebab, Beyti kebab, and shepherd salad. The Iskender kebab was definitely among the best we have tried so far. The flavours were rich, comforting, and beautifully balanced. It was our first time trying Beyti kebab, and we loved it. It was juicy, flavourful, and had that satisfying, homey quality that makes a dish feel especially memorable.
      
      The shepherd salad was very fresh and generously portioned. The tea was brewed perfectly, and the bread was delicious as well. We especially enjoyed the way the bread was served warm and puffed up, full of steam inside — it made the presentation feel fun, fresh, and inviting.
      
      We were so impressed with the food that we ordered their platter for two as takeout before leaving. The platter included two skewers of Adana kebab, one skewer of chicken, one skewer of beef kebab, two pieces of lamb, two individual portions of lahmacun, fries, roasted tomatoes, freshly made bread, rice, chef’s salad, and three dips. It was generous, varied, and a great way to try more of their menu.
      
      We tried the takeout the next day, and it was still very tasty. Even after reheating, the kebabs stayed tender and juicy, which says a lot about the quality and preparation.
      
      The restaurant had a steady lunch crowd, but it did not feel overly busy or chaotic. Service was impressively efficient, and everyone we interacted with was friendly and professional.
      
      Overall, we truly enjoyed our experience at Istanbul Kebab and Donair in Edmonton.
