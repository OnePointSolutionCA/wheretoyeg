      Perfect Saturday morning combo: Maple Latte extra hot and Cranberry Orange Muffin warmed up with thumb of butter 👍
      
      We’ve been keeping an eye on this cute little coffee shop opening up in Chappelle. Finally a soft opening. Staff were friendly. Service was good. Latte was exceptional. Nice silky smooth foam.
      We had Maple Latte which was nicely sweetened. Usually I need to add sweetness to others but Tulip does there’s just right.
      The Blueberry Scone was good. Not too dry like other places. It’s not like other scones. Sorta like a blend of a scone and biscotti. Also I had the Blueberry muffin. It was refrigerated and wrapped with plastic. (Message to owner) Maybe keep out at room temperature. It was moist but muffin top was soggy.
      I’m just nitpicking here. We had a nice time and it is nice to have a coffee shop where neighbors can gather. It was very family friendly. They did a great job for their first day. It was pretty steady. Keep up the good work.
      
      Suggestions for owners:
      
      Maybe some little flowers on the tables. Fake or real. Just for ambience.
      It would be nice to have cream available for customers to doctor their coffee.
      We noticed a lot of customers bringing their dogs, but there was not a doggy water bowl outside. That would be a nice touch.
      B as by chairs are now available 👍
