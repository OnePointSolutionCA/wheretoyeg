      Absolutely delightful experience. I was awestruck by the portion sizes of their regular sized donair for their price, and even more impressed by the flavours and perfected ingredient ratios. The pita was bursting with meat, lettuce, tomatoes and onions, all fresh and coated in their signature sweet sauce. Me and my father dined in and were received by friendly, courteous staff who brought our food to our table on real plates—a pleasant surprise that I did not expect. The onion rings were superb, crispy and golden brown with a batter that didn’t overpower the flavour of the onions whilst still bringing a rich, browned butter like flavour to each mouthful. The atmosphere was casual but welcoming. I felt welcome there. Yet another factor that impressed me was how they prepared the packaging of the donairs. This may sound over the top, but believe me when I say that the plastic bag that comes over your donair to use as a sauce-catcher is ingenious and very, very necessary. Thanks to that bag I escaped a sea of delectable sauce filling my plate and possibly running down my hands and arms. They also provided us with  plentiful napkins, all of which we used. Good food is always messy, and the Famous Donair seems to be acutely aware of this. 10/10 experience. Will dine again.
