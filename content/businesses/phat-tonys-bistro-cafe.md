      Really enjoyed Phat Tony’s! I had the Reuben and it was so good, really flavorful and I loved the Dijon on it. I also had a bite of my son’s butter tart square and that was fabulous too. It looks like a lot of their squares are homemade, which is such a nice touch.
      
      I also really liked the little side salad that came with the sandwich. I had the green salad and it was super fresh and crunchy, which was perfect to break up the heaviness of the Reuben.
      
      The whole place has this kind of nostalgic 90s feel to it that I really liked. It feels very comforting and homey. It’s actually quite a big restaurant, but it gets PACKED. It definitely seems like everyone in Leduc knows about this place because there were lineups out the door and it was hard to find a table. I would definitely recommend getting there early because the parking lot is also insane and finding a spot can be a bit of a mission.
      
      I got a chance to chat with the owner as well and she was so nice and seems really passionate about her business.
      
      Next time I definitely want to try the roast beef or beef dip. Would absolutely recommend!

      A very fresh, loaded sandwich. A little too loaded - it was hard to start eating because the fillings kept slipping out.
      
      I made my own ham and cheese on multigrain with almost all the veggies. Two sauces. It was fresh - soft bread. It came with a pickle spear which I love.
      
      My son had the clubhouse with coleslaw side. He found the coleslaw sweet but I thought it was very well made and enjoyable.
      
      The service was very friendly. I found it confusing to order and get food, as I’ve never been here before (because the hours only really cater to locals who come for lunch). We ordered and she asked our name, which I gave. We got a table but got takeout because we were on a schedule. I went back up and waited and was eventually told that they bring it to us.
      
      So - order at the counter, but table service for delivery.
      
      Sometimes I just want a fresh well-made sandwich. I’ve been wanting to try this place for quite a long time but I am just not in Leduc on weekdays during the morning and lunch. Open on a Saturday, please!
