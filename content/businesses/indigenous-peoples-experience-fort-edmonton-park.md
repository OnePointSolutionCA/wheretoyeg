      The Indigenous Peoples Experience at Fort Edmonton Park is absolutely wondrous. The space is thoughtfully designed and well curated, offering a rich and authentic representation of Indigenous voices, stories, and traditions. The blending of immersive exhibits, oral histories, and multimedia creates a powerful experience that is both educational and deeply moving. This is not just a museum exhibit, it’s a journey of understanding and connection. A must visit for anyone who values culture, history, and truth told in a respectful and engaging way.
      
      It’s a long walk to the far end of the park .. however you can catch a train from one end to the other. This was a meaningful experience. Look for signs, it can be hard to find if you don’t know where to go. If you have time to tour the entire Fort plan for a few hours at least .. lots of walking so wear comfortable shoes.
