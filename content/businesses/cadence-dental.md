      Cadence Dental — A Remarkable Standard of Excellence
      
      There are dental practices where excellence is promised, and others where it is consistently demonstrated. Cadence Dental belongs decidedly to the latter. The professionalism, craftsmanship, patience and genuine kindness of this team deserve special recognition.
      
      DEANA — Dental Assistant
      
      Deana’s attention to detail is extraordinary. Exceptionally conscientious, knowledgeable and communicative, she possesses patience beyond reasonable expectation. She never appears hurried and maintains standards exceeding those of even the fussiest patient. Her pleasant disposition and reassuring manner bring a welcome sense of calm to the dental chair. One suspects she is considerably more demanding of her own workmanship than any patient could ever be!
      
      DR. SYLVIE RENOIR
      
      An exceptional dentist with remarkable expertise in Invisalign and cosmetic dentistry. Her work reflects the precision and discernment of a fine craftsperson. No shortcuts, no unnecessary haste and no compromise of standards. Every finishing detail matters. Equally admirable is her sensitivity to patient anxiety. Calm, considerate and supportive, she inspires confidence that the finished result will receive her utmost attention.
      
      AMANDA
      
      If you require an impression for a dental retainer, ask for Amanda. She has mastered the undertaking! Her precision is outstanding, her efficiency remarkable and her manner engaging. Never rushed, she nevertheless works with admirable confidence and accuracy. She makes you feel that your procedure is as important to her as it is to you. An exceptional combination of technical ability and personal consideration.
      
      BROOKE — Dental Hygienist
      
      Brooke could confront a veritable quagmire of teeth and emerge victorious! Exceptionally capable, confident and efficient, she transforms what might otherwise be an uncomfortable cleaning into a surprisingly pleasant experience. Her reassuring manner removes apprehension, particularly for nervous patients. This is clearly not her first rodeo! I have remarked that she ought to be a dentist herself, so impressive is her competence. A sincere compliment to her considerable abilities.
      
      DR. KRISTINA McKENZIE
      
      Dr. McKenzie combines exceptional accuracy, sound judgment and genuine compassion. Her reluctance to recommend unnecessarily aggressive treatment is particularly reassuring. Her meticulous precision extends across every facet of dentistry, from minor adjustments to intricate restorations. The extraordinary accuracy of her crown margins is but one example of her craftsmanship.
      
      She refuses to settle for work that is merely acceptable. Yet her demanding standards are accompanied by remarkable patience and understanding, especially toward those who fear needles or dental procedures.
      
      A former University of Alberta Pandas hockey player, she brings an athlete’s discipline, teamwork and determination to her profession. One suspects finishing second has never appealed to her. Fortunately, her patients are the ones who finish first!
      
      THE FRONT DESK TEAM
      
      Warm, welcoming, personable and unfailingly courteous, the reception staff make every visit pleasant. They remember names and recognize patients as individuals, not file numbers. Their genuine friendliness establishes the atmosphere of personal consideration found throughout the practice.
      
      FINAL THOUGHTS
      
      Dr. Renoir and Dr. McKenzie demonstrate that outstanding dentistry and genuine compassion belong together. Supported by an exceptional team, they have created an environment where patients can feel confident, respected and comfortable.
      
      For anyone whose previous dental experiences have involved anxiety, disappointment or hurried treatment, Cadence Dental offers a welcome contrast.
      
      Going to the dentist need not be an unpleasant experience.
      
      Excellence requires little advertisement when it is so consistently demonstrated.
      
      Cadence Dental has earned my highest recommendation and sincere gratitude.
