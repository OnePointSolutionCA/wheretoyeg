    comment: |
      Had a chance to visit Objects Café and I really love the concept of combining a flower shop and coffee space—it feels very airy, bright, and inviting. The second floor is especially beautiful, and the huge skylight brings in so much natural light, which makes the whole space feel extra cozy.
      
      The coffee was great! I personally loved that it had a smooth chocolatey aftertaste without being too bitter. They also serve Wild Rose cakes, which I think is a very smart choice—if you’re not making your own pastries, sourcing from one of the best in town is definitely the way to go.
      
      As for the flower shop side, I have to be honest—it didn’t quite meet my expectations. Some of the flowers in the cooler looked a bit tired and like they needed to be refreshed. There also wasn’t anyone at the flower counter to assist, so I wasn’t sure if the baristas are meant to help with that or not.
      
      I also found the retail section a bit overwhelming—there’s quite a lot of merchandise, and some of it felt a little unrelated to the overall concept.
      
      Overall, it’s a very cute and aesthetically pleasing space with great coffee, but I think the floral side could use a bit more attention to really match the vision.
