      The drink I had was fantastic. Service was great. This place is nowhere near my house but I’ve always wanted to try it, I’m glad I did!! Definitely recommend.
      I don’t like too sweet, but I do like flavour and creaminess. and with the helpful recommendation from the man at the counter, I got the Kungfu milktea, 30% sweetness, Cloud topping, with “extra flame”. It was perfect. The “extra flame” is a thick syrup (not too sweet) they line the cup with, which adds a subtle extra sweetness to the drink, especially if you get reduced sugar in the milktea. I LOVED this drink. It was like an elevated and sophisticated version of my go-to creme brulee brown sugar drink from my regular milktea shop. (I love both.) I don’t order my drinks with pearls, so I can’t speak to those.
      
      I also got the creme brulee soft serve (without pearls) as I love creme brulee. The milk tea flavoured ice cream was not very sweet, a little too not-sweet for my liking, and the creme brulee pudding at the bottom was a jello texture which I didn’t expect. This was not my personal preference and I probably won’t order it again, but for what it is and if it sounds like your taste (not very sweet, mix of textures), the quality is good. I would definitely try another flavour/version of soft serve next time.
      
      I enjoyed my drink and service here very much, I totally recommend, and want to come back when I’m in the area again.
