    comment: |
      We drove all the way to St. Albert to support a local business, Barako Bistro, but unfortunately, our overall experience was disappointing.
      
      We were particularly excited to visit because, given the restaurant’s name Barako Bistro, coffee selection seemed quite limited, so offering more coffee varieties, including Barako coffee, would make the experience more worthwhile.
      
      The food presentation was nice, but unfortunately, the taste did not live up to our expectations. The chicken was bland and lacked seasoning, and even the chicken patties tasted burnt and lacked flavour. The atchara was overly sour and acidic, without a good balance of sweet and sour.
      
      We also ordered two BBQ chicken meals with rice, but the chicken appeared to be undercooked. We could still see what looked like uncooked fat, which made us concerned that it had not been properly grilled or cooked through. The staff asked us to returned the chicken, and although the staff took it back, it was neither replaced nor removed from our bill so we end up just eating rice and egg . Instead, we were given a cinnamon bun by the chef but never cared to talked to us about our meal which did not really address the issue.
      
      The lasagna verde was okay, but nothing particularly memorable. The one item we genuinely enjoyed was the pandesal. It had a nice balance of salty and sweet flavours, although it was different from the traditional pandesal we expected. The pandan drink was also disappointing because we could barely taste the pandan flavour.
      
      The restaurant's ambiance was decent, and the food presentation showed some promise, but overall, the food quality and handling of our concerns did not meet our expectations, especially considering the drive to St. Albert.
      
      Unfortunately, based on our experience, we would not recommend Barako Bistro for a full dining experience. Better coffee options, more flavourful food, properly cooked chicken, and better handling of customer concerns would go a long way toward improving the experience. For us, the pandesal was the highlight of the visit, while the rest of the meal left us disappointed.
