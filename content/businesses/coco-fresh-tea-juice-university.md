      I recently visited CoCo Fresh Tea & Juice with high expectations, especially as someone who genuinely enjoys bubble tea. While the overall experience was pleasant, I did leave somewhat disappointed due to an issue with my order.
      
      I had asked for the Mango Dream, a drink I’ve heard great things about and was looking forward to trying. Unfortunately, the staff were unfamiliar with it and seemed unsure of what I was referring to. After a bit of back and forth, they ended up making me something similar, though it wasn’t quite what I had hoped for. The drink I received was okay—it wasn’t bad by any means—but it lacked the vibrant mango flavor and creamy balance I was expecting. It felt like a substitute, not the real thing.
      
      That said, I want to acknowledge that the staff were polite and tried their best to accommodate my request, even if they weren’t fully informed. The store itself was clean, bright, and welcoming, and despite the hiccup with my order, I still enjoyed being there. As always, I appreciate CoCo’s wide selection of drinks and the consistency in the quality of their boba.
      
      While this visit didn’t fully meet my expectations, I still consider CoCo one of my go-to spots for bubble tea. I hope next time the staff will be better informed about the menu items—but I’ll definitely be back to try something else.
      
      They still get the 5 starzzzz!
