      I really wish we could have liked this place. Went tonight and ordered the chicken tenders, fries, and a buffalo chicken wrap.
      
      The chicken tenders: you can’t even call these things chicken tenders. They’re bagged frozen Walmart chicken sticks. Not even real chicken but like chicken meal. Over cooked, dry, almost inedible. The kicker is it was $12.99 for 5 dry awful sticks. The fries were decent but came at a price of $5.99 since the tenders and all meals don’t come with sides.
      
      The buffalo chicken wrap: it was meh. Menu states crispy chicken, it was grilled chicken which is fine, wasn’t buffalo sauce on it, was a watered down hot sauce. Veggies seemed fresh and crispy at least.
      
      Unfortunately I can’t see us returning to this place.
      
      I had went within their opening week as well, and had their ‘smash’ burger. Which again was just okay. We want to support this place as it’s convenient to have in the area but for the price and quality we just can’t be returning.
