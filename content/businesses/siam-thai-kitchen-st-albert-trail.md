      On a rainy Sunday afternoon, we expected a quiet lunch but instead found a packed house, which speaks volumes about how popular Siam Thai has become. Despite being incredibly busy, the service was exceptional from the moment we walked through the doors. The staff were welcoming, attentive, and made us feel genuinely appreciated as guests.
      
      The restaurant itself has a beautiful modern-chic atmosphere that feels both stylish and comfortable. One of the unique touches is that some dishes are delivered by a BellaBot, adding a fun and memorable modern twist to the dining experience. The Bella bot even sang my grandma happy birthday 🎶
      
      As for the food, every dish was a winner.
      The Chicken Green Curry was rich, fragrant, and packed with authentic Thai flavors. The Panang Red Curry with tender beef was absolutely delicious, featuring melt-in-your-mouth morsels of beef in a creamy, perfectly balanced curry sauce. The Tom Yum Soup was outstanding bold, aromatic, and bursting with the perfect combination of spicy, sour, and savory flavors.
      
      The real surprise was the Pad Thai. It was incredibly flavorful, perfectly cooked, and impossible to stop eating. We love our food spicy, so we asked for extra Sriracha and had an absolute blast turning up the heat.
      
      From the warm hospitality and modern ambiance to the excellent food and unique BellaBot service, Siam Thai delivered an experience that exceeded expectations. Even with a full dining room on a rainy Sunday, everything ran smoothly and professionally.
      
      We'll definitely be back the next time we're craving authentic Thai cuisine. Highly recommended for anyone looking for fantastic food, excellent service, and a memorable dining experience in St. Albert. 🌶️🍜🇹🇭
