      It’s been over a year since we last ordered chicken from this SFC location so we decided to give it a try for dinner. In the past we really liked their Korean version of fried chicken but unfortunately things seemed to have changed from what we remember and not necessarily for the better. We ordered their golden kari, garlic soy, cilantro lime and gochujang caramel chicken. We also ordered the mac n’ cheese pesto salad and the house cut fries.
      
      The first thing we noticed was that the pieces of chicken were larger size pieces like what you would find in an order of chicken at KFC or Mary Brown’s. We prefer the smaller cut up chicken pieces that SFC originally used. Then we noted that they appeared to have messed up the sauces in 2 of the chicken orders. The garlic soy tasted like gochujang soy because it had a definite “kick” to it and no garlic. We’ve had garlic soy a number of times in the past and this was definitely not it. Then the gochujang caramel appears to only have caramel sauce, just a sweet sauce with no spiciness. The golden kari and cilantro lime appeared correct and tasted pretty good.
      
      The house cut fries were very good but we felt that there was an excessive amount of bits and crumbs at the bottom of our order.
      
      The mac n’ cheese pesto salad was not as good as it was in the past; the sauce appeared thinner and the pesto not very prominent. We would not order it again.
      
      When picking up our order we noticed that SFC was extremely busy and wonder if their quality control is slipping because of the volume of meals they’re pushing thru their kitchen.
      
      We order online using their website and that process worked very well, we paid at the till when we picked up and the person working there was competent and nice. However, we did have to wait about 15 minutes longer than the scheduled time to get our order. There’s free parking in the shopping centre parking lot but stalls closest to SFC were full.
