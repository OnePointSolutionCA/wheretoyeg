      The Butchery by Rge Road seems to wear a few hats, butcher, baker, but not a candlestick maker.
      
      The reason I say that, they’ve got a small lunch offering, dog bones, and a few other assorted items beyond the meat counter.  Not a huge selection in any category, but that actually adds to the respect factor.  You can tell they’re bringing in whole animals and using every part, and more than likely building those lunch items in-house from what they’ve sourced and prepared themselves.
      
      I’ll admit, I was a little wary of the prices at first.  Then I tried the sausage roll, under six dollars, and probably the meatiest, richest one I’ve had.  Excellent quality.  Followed that up with what I believe was a mortadella sandwich, with some added ingredients, and it was fantastic.  A very finely made mortadella.
      
      Both items were very fairly priced for the quality.  Very impressed.  Glad I walked in today.  I don’t get to this part of the city often, but I’ll make a point of coming back.
