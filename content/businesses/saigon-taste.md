      Nice little family run spot for Vietnamese food. First time trying this location. Ambience was clean and had good decorations. Menu was pretty unique looking and cute. Service was pretty friendly. It was quite busy during dinner time so understandably took a bit of time to get the order as there was only one server. Tried their wings special which came as a good appetizer portion. It was meaty and cooked well, however, it could use more flavour from the fish sauce glaze. A bit more caramelization of the glaze  would probably have enhanced the flavour they were trying to achieve. The Spicy Beef Noodle Soup was pretty good and it came as a large portion. Noodles were cooked well and came with plenty of meat. The soup tasted pretty good and light but personally could use a bit more layers of flavour and spice comparing it to other Vietnamese locations that I’ve tried. Overall, good dinner and nice local spot to support a small business. Would come again to try their other items on the menu.
