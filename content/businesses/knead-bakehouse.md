      I’ve already become a fan of their sourdough, have been enjoying it for the past 2 years.
      
      There is a major difference about properly fermented sourdough. That long fermentation develops incredible flavour and character, and the natural fermentation process involves lactic-acid bacteria and yeast. Whatever the science behind it, the result is unmistakable: depth, flavour and bread that actually tastes alive. You can taste what long fermentation and careful craftsmanship bring to the bread.
      
      But then…
      
      I tried the cinnamon bun for the first time.
      This wasn’t just a cinnamon bun.
      It was THE cinnamon bun. 🤯
      
      The dough was soft, rich and perfectly baked. The butter and sugar were beautifully balanced, and there was heaps of cinnamon exactly where there should be heaps of cinnamon. 😋
      
      And then there was the icing. REAL icing!!!
      
      Not a drizzle.
      
      Not a thin glaze.
      
      A glorious, thick layer of creamy icing.
      
      The kind where you look at the bun and think, “Yep. We’re not pretending to be healthy today.”
      
      Every element worked together perfectly.

      A very lovely family-owned bakery! The owners and staff are incredibly kind, welcoming, and clearly put a lot of care into what they do. Everything is baked fresh that morning, and you can really taste the difference.
      
      They’ve become very popular very quickly, so it’s not surprising that some items sell out early. They’ve been very transparent about their hours and that they may sell out, and I think it’s important to keep in mind that this is a small family business with limited staff and space. I’ve personally been in line and seen people purchasing large quantities of popular items, so it’s understandable that things can sell out quickly.
      
      Be prepared for a possible line up—it’s worth it! ❤️ I’d much rather support a small family business that makes everything with natural ingredients than a corporation.
      
      Wishing this wonderful family business lots of success! They deserve all the support. Their pretzels and cinnamon buns are the best! ❤️
