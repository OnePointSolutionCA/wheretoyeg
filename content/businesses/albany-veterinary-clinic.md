A box of tissues ready to be used, which I really needed. Dr. Cecilia came in and explained everything again, I signed the paperwork and she took my pup to the back to set up his IV. She shortly came back with him and asked me if I was ready for the medication to be administered. They normally mail it out, but I was coming in with our other pup just a couple of days later to get his teeth cleaned, so I asked if I could have it then. It was such a lovely gesture and my kids especially were so grateful for the little paw prints that they made for us.
