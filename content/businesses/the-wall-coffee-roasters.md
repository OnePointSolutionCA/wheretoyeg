  - name: "Kelsey Chow"

    comment: |
      I went to check out this place using my 2026 Coffee Pass and wow! You'd never guess there would be such a lovely and charming roastery and cafe in the middle of the industrial area. The building is so light, bright, and airy with big windows to let in the sunshine, white washed walls, and warm wood accents.
      I upgraded to a latte and the cinnamon honey was delicious.
      I didn't stop for a bite to eat, but it seemed like a good selection of light fare and snacks were available.
      I assume this is a common place to work as well as gather, as the one space on the "quieter side" had ample plug ins and lights should they be necessary.
      There is a room full of both coffee related merch as well as branded apparel which I perused quickly while waiting.
      The best seats in the house may be the cozy hanging egg chairs, complete with cushions and a low table for drinks and your snacks.
      This place is worth the stop if you're in the area or passing through. It would be a great place to work or share some quality time. I will definitely be back sometime to enjoy another coffee and a snack, but as it’s quite out of the way from my typical routes, it might be a little while before I make it back.
