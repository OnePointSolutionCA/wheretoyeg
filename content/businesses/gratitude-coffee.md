      Gratitude Coffee — Sherwood Park
      
      A Place You Don’t Rush Through
      
      There are places you visit… and there are places that gently invite you to stay.
      
      I walked into Gratitude Coffee expecting a good cup of coffee. What I found instead was something quieter—almost like stepping into a different pace of life. Not slower in a lazy sense, but slower in a meaningful one.
      
      The kind of place that reminds you you don’t always have to rush.
      
      The Welcome
      
      Before the coffee, there were the people.
      
      Not polished. Not scripted. Just genuinely warm.
      
      You can tell when kindness is part of the culture, not just part of the training—and that’s exactly what you feel here. A welcome that doesn’t push, doesn’t perform… just receives you as you are.
      
      The Cup
      
      I ordered a latte.
      
      I won’t pretend I can name every note or origin—but I know when something is right. This was one of those cups. Smooth, balanced, with a tone that felt unique enough to notice… and memorable enough to slow you down mid-sip.
      
      It didn’t just wake me up—it made me pause.
      
      The Space
      
      And then, the space itself.
      
      Wide. Open. Thoughtfully laid out.
      
      This is easily one of the most spacious coffee shops I’ve stepped into. There’s room for conversations, room for quiet work, room for reflection. You can bring a friend—or come alone and not feel alone.
      
      It’s the kind of place where time stretches just enough for you to catch your breath.
      
      A Quiet Reflection
      
      There’s a line from C. S. Lewis that came to mind while sitting here:
      
      “We are what we believe we are.”
      
      Places like this gently shape that belief.
      
      Not loudly. Not forcefully. But by creating environments where gratitude, presence, and connection are possible again.
      
      A Word of Encouragement
      
      To the team at Gratitude Coffee—
      
      It’s clear this place was built with intention.
      
      What started as something simple has become something that now holds people—hosts conversations, creates rest, and reminds a community to slow down and notice life again.
      
      You’ve chosen authenticity over efficiency. Presence over pressure. Depth over noise.
      
      That matters more than you know.
      
      Keep building this kind of space. Keep welcoming people the way you do. Keep holding that quiet standard of excellence and sincerity.
      
      You’re not just serving coffee.
      
      You’re shaping moments people carry with them long after they leave.
