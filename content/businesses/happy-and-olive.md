      Happy & Olive, Edmonton, West End / 142 Street area offers one of the more original and thoughtfully styled dining experiences in the city. With memorable flavour combinations, beautiful presentation, and a menu that goes beyond breakfast alone, it feels creative, polished, and distinctly its own.
      
      What makes it stand out is how intentional everything feels. The food is creative without becoming fussy, the room is stylish without losing warmth, and the overall experience carries that rare owner-touched feeling. There is a sense that details matter here — not just in the menu, but in the way the whole place has been imagined.
      
      A special part of the visit was speaking with Andrea and learning about her deep roots in the Edmonton culinary community as a restaurateur. It is genuinely encouraging to see someone with that depth of experience bringing their best to Edmonton’s restaurant scene through flavour, design, hospitality, and care.
      
      This is also one of the more family-considerate places I have come across. The bathroom is beautiful, accessible, and includes a change station that feels genuinely well thought out. For parents, that kind of attention can make a real difference. Altogether, Happy & Olive feels like a place helping lead the way in Edmonton when it comes to restaurant design, creativity, and a more inclusive, welcoming dining experience.
      
      Easily accessible from Whitemud West and well worth the trip from elsewhere, it is the kind of place that feels memorable not only for the food, but for the care behind the whole experience.

      - Meaningful Edmonton restaurant roots

      - Accessible washroom access
      - Looking forward to return
