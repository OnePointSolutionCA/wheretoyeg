      Stumbled upon this place while walking around and did not realize it was actually a specialty shop focused on allergy friendly products.
      
      They have a huge selection of gluten free sweets and baked goods, along with lots of options for people avoiding eggs, nuts, or dairy. Even though I can eat gluten, I still really enjoyed their peanut butter cookies. They were soft and somehow tasted healthy while still being genuinely good.
      
      There is no seating, so it is mainly a takeout spot. You just go in, grab what you need, and head out. There are parks and benches nearby though, so it is easy to sit outside and eat.
      
      It is definitely a bit pricey, but that is expected for a specialty store like this. Overall, I was really happy with the experience and thought it was a cool find.
