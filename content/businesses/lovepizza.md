      This is one of the best pizzas you're going to buy in Spruce Grove. The value is quite good, similar to Dominos, cheaper than Royal, better than both 😂 I love the other places to, but this place is quickly becoming my preferred choice.
      It is incredibly well priced for what you get. I ordered a large and a signature and it fed me and my wife and my 20-year-old son with some leftovers and it was 50 bucks. The individual or signature size is easily enough to split between one person as a shared. I would recommend getting a couple and you'll probably have a little left over. The variety of choices are similar to other classic staples (meat lovers, cheese, etc), but they definitely have some unique twists. I would recommend the ode to Ukraine and the tropical BBQ myself if you're wanting to try something a little different but amazing.
      
      The service has been outstanding on every occasion I've been there so far.
      
      If I had to make a single recommendation and it's not even a critique would be to get some dessert that is different than the generic desserts typically offered by most pizza places... Something to match the unique Vibe of your pizzas 😉
      
      But overall, this is easily one of the best places for pizza in Spruce Grove.
