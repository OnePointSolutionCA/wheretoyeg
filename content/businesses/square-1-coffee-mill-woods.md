      As always, I was pleasantly surprised to see a new Square 1 had popped up in the new medical building in Millwoods, at 7319-29th Ave. I had to wait for someone having an appointment so it was the perfect chill stop. I observed many people coming and going during my waiting time at Square 1, so seems to be a new and popular coffee spot! The two servers were efficient and got my order quickly. I love a good solid Square 1 decaf coffee with cream, since it’s never bitter. The ambience is restful and there is some seating in the restaurant but also through out the whole medical building, lots of places to sit and sip. Their morning glory muffin was moist and delicious and I’m a butter person, so I found it very moist and delish. The decor in the cafe was lovely, minimalist but with beautiful artwork gracing the walls. Highly recommend you stop in if you happen to be here and even if you don’t! It’s worth the drive to enjoy the upscale feel and a top class coffee experience.
