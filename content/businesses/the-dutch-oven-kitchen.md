      I don’t even know where to begin because this place absolutely blew me away from the second I walked through the door. The smell alone is enough to make your stomach instantly growl — even if you weren’t hungry before, you WILL be after stepping inside. Everything smelled so fresh, rich, savory, and unbelievably delicious!
      
      Their menu is so unique and thoughtfully crafted, and every single thing we tried was a 10/10. I ordered the fried chicken sandwich and I genuinely could not believe the size of it — this thing was GINORMOUS! Perfectly cooked, crispy, juicy, loaded with flavor, and the arugula added such a fresh, delicious balance that made the whole sandwich feel elevated and incredibly well put together. And their sauces?! Absolutely amazing.
      
      I also ordered the garlic parmesan fries and I can honestly say they were the BEST garlic parm fries I’ve ever had. The flavor was bursting in the most perfect way — nothing was overpowering, everything was beautifully balanced, delicate, rich, and absolutely delectable. You could tell real care went into every bite.
      
      My young daughter had the chicken fingers and poutine, and she was SO happy with her meal too. The chicken fingers were generously sized, crispy, and delicious. The poutine was fantastic — the gravy was excellent, the cheese was great quality, and I especially appreciated that the gentleman kindly packed the gravy on the side so the fries wouldn’t get soggy before we got home. That small thoughtful gesture honestly says so much about the level of care and customer service here.
      
      The portions are HUGE. I am unbelievably full and still can’t even finish everything. My sandwich and fries alone are easily two meals worth of food! If you’ve got an appetite, you absolutely will NOT be disappointed.
      
      Amazing food, incredible flavors, huge portions, thoughtful service, and an unforgettable experience from start to finish. We will 100% be back!

      Well what a treat that was! We had to go to Spruce Grove for an appointment and were in need of a decent meal while we were there. It seemed that all we were going to find was fast food but we saw a listing for this little gem and scooched over, fingers crossed, hoping for the best. What a delight! I impulsively added a soup because soup is my love language and I'm so glad I did! The tomato meatball soup was delicious!! It was thick, texturally pleasing and piping hot. The chicken sandwich was hot, obviously homemade and fresh as well as super messy and so flavorful. My husband loved his Yorkshire and Brisket. He commented that the portion seemed small but then added that it was more filling than he expected and that it beat whatever he could have gotten at McDs for the same $$. Overall a fantastic experience, we're driving home today with full bellies and a new favorite spot to stop by on our next trip!
