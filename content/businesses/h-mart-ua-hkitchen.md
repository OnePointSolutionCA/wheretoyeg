      Checked it out for the first time. Was surprised to see it in H-mart tucked away. In fact there's multiple places to eat.
      
      Ordering was Sorta of confusing. There's mostly bimimbap, bulgogi, pancakes and fried Chicken. But then there's also soup meals. You order those separately on a machine. While the other dishes are ordered at till.
      
      Wait time was approximately 20 mins.
      They had no drinks. You can buy drinks in the grocery store to drink there. The tickets are announced electronically but its hard to hear further back into the restaurant over the music.
      Staff was friendly when asked for recommendations.
      
      The location is cute though! I loved the decor. It's felt cozy.  The ceiling especially was good.
      
      The washrooms are locked. You must ask for the key.
      
      The good was well cooked. It was strange seeing how it came out. They came out in take out boxes while others we noticed  got soups in plates and trays. I think the two types of food are independent of each other. So they are served differently. We didn't get napkins with the meal unfortunately. So had to ask.
      
      The chicken was cooked to perfection. Good breading size and not overly sauced. Just the right amount in my opinion to eat. We had the special sauce. It was light and mild. The mala spicy which was rated as super hot was a good balance. Hot enough to taste but not hot enough to numb your taste buds.
      The cheese balls were lighter then expected and great texture. Not salty either.
      
      Overall good experience. Just wished for a few small improvements.
