      This café is definitely unique, and I really appreciate places that try something different. It makes the whole experience feel more memorable and worth going out of your way for.
      
      One of the highlights is their matcha and coffee flights, which are actually quite generous in size, so you get decent value for what you pay. I ordered one of each, and the total came to about $36, which felt relatively reasonable considering how much you get.
      
      That said, being completely honest, the drinks themselves were nothing particularly special. After trying a lot of coffee and matcha over the years, these didn’t stand out as amazing, they were just okay.
      
      Even so, I still think this place is worth visiting for the concept alone. I would come back again to give it another shot and try some different drinks.
