      As fans of coffee flights, BE Coffee had been on our list to try, so we stopped by for morning coffee and were glad we did.
      
      We ordered a cappuccino and the BE Honey coffee flight. The presentation of the flight was one of the highlights of the visit. It came on a honeycomb-style board with the drink names written beside each cup, which was both thoughtful and practical. With flights, it is easy to forget which flavour is which, so this made the tasting experience much easier and more enjoyable.
      
      We also really liked the cups used for the hot drinks. The double-walled glass cups were comfortable to hold even when the drinks were hot, and the transparent design made the coffee feel more visually inviting. It is a small detail, but it adds to the overall sensory experience of enjoying the colour, aroma, and flavour of the coffee.
      
      The BE Honey flight was fun to try, and our personal ranking was cardamom, lavender, honey, and then caramel. The cardamom was especially memorable and gave the flight a more distinctive character.
      
      One thing we really appreciated is that you can choose your own flight selection if you prefer. That makes the menu feel more interactive and allows customers to tailor the experience to their own taste.
      
      The decor is also very nicely done and fits the theme beautifully. The honeycomb and bee-inspired elements feel original without being overdone. I especially liked the ceiling decor, as many cafés either leave the ceiling very plain or go with an unfinished look. Here, the hive-style details and the large wooden bee added personality and made the space feel more intentional.
      
      The service was respectful and efficient, though it could have felt a little warmer. A more cheerful or welcoming interaction would add a lot to the overall vibe, especially in a café with such a charming concept. That said, everyone can have a busy or off day, and it would not stop us from returning.
      
      Overall, BE Coffee is a creative and memorable café with thoughtful presentation, interesting drinks, and a very charming atmosphere. We would definitely come back, and next time we would love to try the matcha flight.
