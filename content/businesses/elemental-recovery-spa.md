      I recently visited Elemental Recovery Spa and overall had a great experience. The facility is beautiful, especially the change rooms, which are thoughtfully equipped with robes, towels, wet-bag storage for bathing suits, combination-lock lockers, feminine hygiene products, a Dyson blow dryer, GHD straightener, and high-quality shampoo and conditioner.
      
      The hot and cold pools offered a significant temperature contrast, which I really enjoyed, and the turmeric tea was a nice touch. I also appreciated the convenient location just off the Henday and close to local shops.
      
      A few things could elevate the experience even further. The front lobby could benefit from some music, especially since treatment rooms are nearby and conversations can carry. The first entrance to the change room is quite dark and could use better lighting. I would have liked the spa music to be louder throughout the relaxation area, and it would be amazing if the dry sauna allowed water and essential oils on the rocks.
      
      One thing we were unsure about was the etiquette around conversation versus silence, as there wasn’t much guidance provided. While I enjoyed the experience, I personally find $79 for a 2-hour weekend visit a bit expensive and would be more likely to return at that price point if it were a private experience.
      
      Overall, it’s a beautiful and well-maintained spa with excellent amenities, and I’d rate it 4/5 stars.
