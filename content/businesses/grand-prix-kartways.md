      As a huge F1 fan, this was an absolute must-do while in Las Vegas, and it exceeded my expectations.
      
      My wife and I spent more than three hours at Grand Prix Plaza between the F1 Drive karting experience and the F1 X attraction. We wrapped up the visit with a cocktail at the bar, which was a great way to end the afternoon.
      
      If you're planning to visit, I highly recommend buying tickets in advance. It lets you secure your preferred time slot and avoids disappointment. We visited on a Monday, and guests purchasing tickets on-site were facing waits of around 40 minutes for kart sessions. The simulators appeared to have much shorter wait times.
      
      The standout attraction for me was F1 X. We took our time and didn't rush through it. Even as an avid Formula 1 fan, I was surprised by how much I learned. There was a great mix of racing history, technical explanations, and interactive exhibits. Some of the content was familiar, but plenty of it was new and genuinely interesting.
      
      The karting experience was also a lot of fun, especially knowing you're driving portions of the actual Las Vegas Grand Prix circuit. One thing to keep in mind is that part of the track is outdoors. If you're sensitive to the Vegas heat, consider booking an evening session when temperatures are more comfortable.
      
      Whether you're a lifelong Formula 1 fan or just curious about the sport, Grand Prix Plaza is well worth dedicating a morning or afternoon to during your Vegas trip.
