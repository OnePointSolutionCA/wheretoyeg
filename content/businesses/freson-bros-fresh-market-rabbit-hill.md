      I shopped here for the first time this evening; it was their huge Alberta flag that caught my attention. I was very impressed with the quality and selection of produce, meats and home goods. That was a huge disappointment for me. I definitely would have given five stars if they packed my groceries.
      
      Give the buying customer the whole experience that fits your market. When people are spending more money on items they can get cheaper else where, you need to set yourself aside from those cheaper stores by making the paying customer feel good about spending their money at your establishment. A way to do that would be to pack their items at check out.
