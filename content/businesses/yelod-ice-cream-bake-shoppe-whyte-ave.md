      Yelo'd has grown far beyond its roots as a niche Filipino ice cream shop and has established itself as one of Edmonton's most innovative dessert destinations. The menu celebrates Filipino flavors while continually introducing creative new offerings, making every visit feel fresh and exciting.
      
      Their ice cream is consistently rich, flavorful, and thoughtfully crafted, but the experience doesn't stop there. Their drinks and pastries are equally impressive and complement the ice cream perfectly.
      
      Our personal favorites are the Halo-Halo, lychee soft serve, Grandpa Wong ice cream and the Champorado Sundae, all showcase the creativity that sets Yelo'd apart. We visit at least three times a month...sometimes even weekly...and there's always something new worth trying. Few places manage to combine quality, creativity, and consistency as well as Yelo'd. It's easily one of Edmonton's best dessert spots.
