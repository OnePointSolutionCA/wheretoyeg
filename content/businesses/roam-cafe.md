      This cafe has it's own space in this building complex, so from the outside it looks fairly hidden. My boyfriend and I just entered through the patio side to get in.
      
      My boyfriend got an Iced Matcha and I ordered an Iced Lavender Latte with oat milk. Service was pretty quick. There weren't many people inside anyways. We came in the afternoon about two hours before closing.
      
      When I first saw our drinks, my first impression was that our drinks were smaller than I thought lol. Also, my latte was slightly too sweet but that's probably because of the lavender syrup. I knew it would get better when the ice would melt and after stirring my drink. Both of our drinks were delicious. I loved how smooth my latte was.
      
      I love the ambiance of the cafe. There's many seats and tables to work. I really liked the decor and furniture inside, it created this eclectic and cool vibe inside. There's large windows facing the patio for natural lighting. Their space inside is very cozy, cool, and pretty. And it's actually quite spacious. I definitely recommend this place.
