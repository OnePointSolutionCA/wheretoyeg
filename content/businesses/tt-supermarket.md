      T&T has a great selection and you can find almost everything you need here. The vegetables are often very fresh, and the seafood section is also great — the clams we bought were big and meaty.
      
      I especially like the bakery section. There’s a huge variety of breads and pastries, and their birthday cakes are very reasonably priced. The Black Forest cake is one of my favorites!
      
      The biggest downside is parking. The parking lot is very tight and it can be really hard to find a spot on weekends. Luckily, cars move in and out pretty quickly, so if you wait a little, you can usually get one. The store itself can also get extremely crowded on weekends.
      
      Overall, a great Asian supermarket with lots of choices, fresh food, and a really good bakery. Definitely recommend!
