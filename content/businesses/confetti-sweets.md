But we also tried the Oreo/cookies and cream cupcake, the coconut cupcake with coconut buttercream with cookies and cream cheese icing, the chocolate cupcake with chocolate buttercream,  and the red velvet cupcake with cream cheese icing. Very moist and rich cupcakes.
      In the past I’ve bought the cookies that were pre baked in the freezer section and these were actually also very tasty and a great idea so to with your extra cookies. And I’ve tried the kits as well and always enjoyed the results.
