      You can't go wrong with a bubble tea from Coco. I love that this chain now has a store in Manning Town Centre. While the Greisbach one isn't far away you can't go wrong with more right! But overall having a bubble tea option finally in Manning is lovely! They provide creative tea drinks such as fresh tea, milk tea, fruit tea, yogurt drinks, and coffee. What I get every time is bubble tea.
      We are big fans of the Taro milk tea flavor ($6.50) and this one did not disappoint. We also ordered the avocado smoothie ($7.30) which was absolutely divine! So creamy and smooth. I added brown sugar Bobas which costs an extra $1. But they are so good!
      And we also tried the lemon probiotic ($7.40) and added the mango popping bobas ($0.70) which was zippy and refreshing. I'm a big fan of the ability to decide how much sugar and ice are in my drink so using the option is grand.
      It was a busy evening that we went with several people in line and behind us. But the service was pretty quick and we were out within 15-20 minutes. Definitely seemed like they could use more staff since the person that placed our order was also busy trying to make drinks as they went too.
      A cute space though that was brightly lit, and pretty clean considering how mucky it was with the snow outside. The little claw arcade games are a fun idea too.
