      BBQ Fit for The King 👑🍖🔥
      
      Smoked meats, huge portions, and Elvis vibes — we loved this BBQ find 😍🔥
      
      Holy smokes!! If you’re looking for some seriously good smoked meats like ribs, chicken, and brisket… you have to try this restaurant we found 🤤🍖.
      The place is so unique, and as soon as you walk in you can already smell the smoked meat — it instantly makes you hungrier lol 😂. If you love Elvis Presley, this place is definitely for you 🎶✨
      
      It’s a counter-service setup — you order and pay at the cashier, and they bring the food to your table. The server was also the cashier and seemed to be doing everything around, but they were super nice too 💕
      
      We got the chicken hot wings 🥵🌶️ — super spicy and jumbo-sized! You can really taste that they’re smoked too. 10/10 🔥
      My husband ordered the chicken and ribs, and we didn’t expect they’d serve half a chicken lol 😂 It was smoked, so tender, falling off the bone, and the meat literally melts in your mouth 🤤🔥
      
      Holy smokes again!! The 1/2 rack of ribs are absolutely deadly 😭🔥 — insanely tender and falling off the bone. The brisket is on fire… 100000/10.
      It says the serving is for one person, but honestly it looks like it could feed two 😅 Huge portion and super worth the price. It even comes with fries — served on a separate plate because everything wouldn’t fit on one 🍟
      
      The fries were so crispy (not soggy at all 😍🍟) and well seasoned — not just ordinary salt lol 😂 and they give a crazy amount.
      He also had the potato salad, which was alright too. The cornbread was a bit dry for me, and the coleslaw and beans were just okay.
      The only thing we didn’t love was the blue cheese dip — but that’s on us since we forgot to ask for ranch 😅
      
      Also not sure if their iced tea is homemade, but it tastes really good. They have sweetened and unsweetened options and it’s refillable — always a win 🥤
      
      Overall, such a fun BBQ spot with smoky, tender meats and generous portions. Definitely worth the visit if you’re a BBQ lover (and especially if you love Elvis) 😍🔥🍖
