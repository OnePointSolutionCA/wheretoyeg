      Boualouang Laos & Thai Cuisine is a true gem in Edmonton’s Chinatown! I’ve been craving authentic Southeast Asian flavors, and this family-run spot delivered beyond expectations. From the moment we walked in, the warm, inviting atmosphere and friendly staff made us feel right at home.
      We started with the zesty papaya salad – perfectly spicy and refreshing – followed by the aromatic Tom Yum soup that had just the right balance of lemongrass, lime, and heat. The green curry was creamy, flavorful, and packed with fresh veggies and tender chicken, while the Pad Thai was spot-on: tangy, nutty, and loaded with shrimp. We finished with mango sticky rice for dessert, which was sweet, sticky perfection.
      Everything tasted fresh, homemade, and truly authentic – like a trip to Thailand and Laos without leaving the city. Portions are generous, prices are fair, and the service was attentive even on a busy night. I’ve tried many Thai places in Edmonton, but Boualouang stands out as the top choice. Highly recommend – we’ll be back soon!
