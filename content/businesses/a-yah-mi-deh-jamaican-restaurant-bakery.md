      Fried dumpling 9.5/10
      
      There was a healthy amount of rice plated at the side of the chicken, it did not cover the bottom of the box. The texture of the rice is a bit different than I am used to.
      
      Soup: More of a thinner consistency than a thick one. Generously portioned with plenty of meat and food. The flavour is there but could be increased.
      
      Jerk chicken: Not your authentic jerk chicken and could be a bit more flavourful. However, it was still enjoyable to have.
      
      Fried chicken: Nicely flavoured and I enjoyed the level of crispness. The size of the chicken was generous and I would order this again.
      
      Coleslaw: This tasted like they added seasoning or used a dressing that is close to tasting like coleslaw but not quite.
      
      Fried dumpling: This tasted so good. Nicely flavoured, soft, and actually delicious. I would definitely order this again.
