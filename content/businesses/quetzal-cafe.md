      We recently tried this new local spot and had high hopes because the photos online looked really good and inviting. The staff were kind and welcoming, which we appreciated.
      The coffee was great, and the price was reasonable — we paid about $5 for coffee and bread, which is fair. However, the bread was hard. I’m not sure if that’s how it’s traditionally served, but it wasn’t something I enjoyed.
      The enchilada was okay, nothing special but decent. The biggest disappointment was the noodles..it tasted like microwaved instant noodles with some beef and bit of greens, and at $10, it didn’t feel worth the price.
      Overall, we were really excited to try this place, but the experience fell short for us. Maybe we just ordered the wrong items, but for a first visit, it didn’t leave a great impression. maybe it’s just not for us.
