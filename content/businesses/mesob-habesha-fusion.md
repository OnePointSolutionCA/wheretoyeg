      Having only enjoyed Ethiopian cuisine one other time, this place was an absolute delight, and it far exceeded our expectations.
      
      This place put on an absolute exhibition with its main course (Chacha Tibs over a Veggie/Beyanyrtu Combo) and Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony to follow.
      
      The elaborate spread for our main course was delightful on all sensory levels: the fragrant spiced meats, a colourful display of vegetables, the soft, tactile, ASMR sensations of ripping the tissue-thin bread, gripping chunks of food and feeding each morsel into the mouth. Savouring every bite and the different flavour combinations each handful conjured up.
      
      We were blown away in the second course as the helpful staff demonstrated how to ‘activate’ our freshly brewed coffees.  Each pour of the hot drink was fun, with fresh popcorn to enjoy on the side and smoky incense as we chatted the night away.
      
      Between the dining experience and an unexpected greeting by an entire table of strangers dining at another table, we felt warmth from both the food and the people at this fine establishment.  If you are new to this style of eating, our suggestion is to wholeheartedly embrace it with an open mind. Unforgettable!
