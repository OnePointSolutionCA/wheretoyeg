      The lack of value at this place is unreal.
      I have been to many pho restaurants during my time here in Edmonton, and this is the least impressive one yet. The portions are very small, and basic hospitality is nonexistent. They are incredibly stingy with essentials like napkins and sauce; I was given one measly napkin with my order, and when I asked for a paper towel, the waiter gave me exactly one sheet from a roll.
      
      The bowl I had was a large which seems like a small in other restaurants, the pho itself wasn't too bad, good broth but the noodles are chewy and slimy with no texture.
      
      The green onion cake portions are tiny compared to other local spots. I finished my sweet chili sauce quickly, but the waiter never checked on me after dropping off the food.
      
      Unlike 90% of Vietnamese restaurants in the city, they don't serve tea to start, and even the side of bean sprouts and lime was bare.
      
      The biggest issue was the bill. My order of a large beef pho and green onion cakes should have totaled $21.80, but I was charged $38.00 and I tipped 10% which totalled $41. When I asked for a receipt, the staff fumbled with the printer and eventually handed me their merchant copy as it wasn't printing. Outside, I realized they had charged me for an extra order of fish sauce chicken wings ($17.95). When I went back inside, they claimed they were "just about to come find me," which was hard to believe as they hadn't moved from the register. They refunded me $12.00 in cash and insisted it was the correct amount, even though they still owed me more. I eventually just gave up and left.
      
      This is a shameless establishment with subpar food and frugal practices. This was my first and LAST time visiting
