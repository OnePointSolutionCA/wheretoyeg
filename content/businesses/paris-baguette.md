    comment: |
      The prices are reasonable.
      The coffee was delicious.
      
      There are a lot of bakery options, mostly European and Asian style bakeries. Most of the items are sweet, but they offer a wide variety of unique and tasty choices.
      
      Especially the jumbo twisted donuts, they taste just like authentic Asian style pastries.
      
      They also seem have huge variety when it comes to cakes. The variety and presentation are both impressive.
      
      You select the products yourself using a single use tong and place them on a tray lined with parchment paper, then bring everything to the cashier where the total is calculated and the purchase is completed.
      
      Even though we went on a Sunday 11am, we didn’t have to wait in line that long. The staff were friendly.
      
      Parking is a bit difficult here. There are paid parking spots available across the street near and around the shop.
