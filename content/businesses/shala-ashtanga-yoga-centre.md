      Shala Ashtanga Yoga Centre is the first review we’ve ever done of a traditional Ashtanga yoga studio and a Mysore class at that (a self-led yoga class where students practice independently at their own pace). As soon as we found out we were doing that we were like…uhhhhhh. Even though I’ve been practising for over 15 years and we go to a squillion yoga classes, we were still intimidated by having to lead our own practice. BUT – turns out there’s no need to fret. Going to a Mysore class as a beginner to Ashtanga might actually be the greatest thing ever and this hidden sanctuary in the middle of downtown Edmonton is one of the greatest places to learn. The Shala is small but beautifully and intentionally put together and they're in line with their yoga competitors in Edmonton in terms of price. Absolutely worth a visit.
