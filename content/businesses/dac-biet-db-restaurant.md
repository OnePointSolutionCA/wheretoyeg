      I visited Edmonton Valley Zoo and happened to discover this newly opened restaurant not too far from there. What a lovely find!
      
      The space is bright, spacious, and very comfortable. I really appreciated how the tables are arranged smartly, with enough room to move chairs comfortably while still having privacy for conversation.
      The menu is also very thoughtful, with English, Vietnamese, and Chinese options, which is great for Asian clients and visitors. The food feels authentic, with a strong Huế-style Vietnamese flavour. Overall, the food was very good. I wouldn’t say “wow,” but definitely solid, tasty, and worth coming back for.
      
      I also really liked the ambience. Everything felt clean, fresh, and welcoming, even the washroom was very clean and smelled nice, which I truly appreciate.
      
      Overall, a very good experience. I would recommend this restaurant, especially if you are visiting the Edmonton Valley Zoo area and looking for a comfortable place to enjoy authentic Vietnamese food.
