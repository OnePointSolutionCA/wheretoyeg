      TL;DR Passionate knowledgeable employees, immaculately kept space and cabinets, large selection of games, refreshments and water, great vibes and awesome community. Plenty of space to rest, couch/chairs with nice cushions, good prices. Free to play old school cab, Love it!

Anyone who likes arcades should give it a go, and for those who specifically like rhythm gaming, this place is for you! Seriously though, it has amazing vibes, there's lots of room to take a break between games with seating like couches and chairs, and the employees and staff are always kind and happy to help! The game selection is definitely really good, there's plenty of different games for everyone, and the prices are also really good too! It's easy to lose a few hours here for sure.
      
      They also sell refreshments, and have a water cooler so you can stay hydrated and playing! They have a really cool feature where they utilize white-boards and magnets for que lines for some of the popular cabinets, which ensures everyone gets a fair chance to play. The people who are there who arnt employees I've found to always be a delight to be around as well, and it's a very accepting and welcoming community. Everyone is very knowledgeable, and as I stated before the staff are kind, and definitely know what they are doing!
      
      A specific employee helped someone and myself out very quickly and efficiently as well at one point which Id love to point out!
