    comment: |
      Tried this place for dim sum lunch this past Saturday 2026-07-11. Overall it was good but not spectacular. The reviews vary widely in Google. My rating would be about 7/ 10. If size matters, you will like this place.
      
      The har gows ( steamed shrimp dumplings: 7/10) and the wu koks ( deep fried taro puffs: 7/ 10) were huge, about 2X the size of other restaurants. The stuffing were average and the har gow wrapping were stuck together and tore when adjacent dumpling was picked up.
      
      The steamed curried squid comprises reconstituted dried squid. The sauce and squid were flavourful. (8/10)
      
      The signature chow mein was good, crunchy noodles were deep fried and came with a good mix of toppings: shrimp, scallop, squid, chicken, char siu, and yu choy. Ample flavourful sauce accompanied the toppings (8/10).
      
      The mini egg tarts were disappointing. It looked OK, the pastry was layered & puffy, but the egg custard was dry/ chewy. Fail. (2/10)
      
      The beef cheung fun was good, stuffing was tasty and the rice crepe texture was fine. No pic. (7/10)
      
      Restaurant interior is new, brightly lit and tables are spaced well apart. Location is in an area where many homeless people frequent. We did not encounter any issues.
      
      Service was acceptable. Food orders arrived about all within 20 mins of ordering. There were staff standing around who could be more helpful.
      
      As a visitor, I would seek other dim sum place before coming here. There used to be better choices. Suggestions welcomed.
