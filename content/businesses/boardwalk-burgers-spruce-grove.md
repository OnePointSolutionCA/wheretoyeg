      Amazing. My first time there today! I had their new "Heatwave" burger (double) with sweet potato fries and a fountain drink. To say the burger was just good, would be a huge understatement. It was delicious!!!
      
      The burger was so hot all the way through, it was really fresh! It did not taste like it had been sitting there waiting to be served, it was actually made to order.
      
      There was good amount of cheese on the burger and the onions and jalapeños were excellent. Ask for extra jalapeños if you like as many as I do because there was about 5-6 decent sized jalapeños on there, but I like a serious LAYER of jalapeños. All around it was a super tasty burger and incredibly filling! The patties are thick!
      
      The sweet potato fries were excellent and the Chipotle sauce is the standard Chipotle sauce you'd expect with sweet potatoe fries.
      
      Definitely coming back! Also I looked at their kids meals, I'm definitely bringing my kids here too
      
      Their dining area is wonderful. It's clean, well maintained, the music is perfect - not too loud, good mix of older style country. Heard some Johnny Cash and Dolly Parton. It was a nice comfortable and relaxing setting where you can truly enjoy your meal.
      
      Lastly their customer service was excellent. I hope I'm spelling his name right - Uivesh at the front counter was very polite and friendly. He brought the food to the table, kindly checked in and asked how everything was and he was all around an exceptional worker who went over and above for the customer satisfaction. Well done sir. Thank you.
      
      Edit to add: I did bring my kids here later on in the week. They LOVED IT! My son said to me that this is his new favourite restaurant. You definitely gained some regulars now! Also I had the new "Boardwalk" burger that time... not much of a fan of that one though, but it's ok, I'm working my way through the whole menu. We're still coming back.
