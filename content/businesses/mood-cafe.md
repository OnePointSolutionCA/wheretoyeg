  - name: "T-Bird"
    rating: 2
    comment: |
      We had drinks and treats on a Friday about 3 pm.  It's on a pretty busy corner in Belgravia with angle parking for about 6-8 cars.  It's a fairly large place with lots of inside tables and an outside patio, where we sat.
      
      Ordering is confusing.  They have a till at the corner of the food/drink area, but no directions. There's an ice cream display and a pastry display.  But ... customers seemed to just stand and wait to be recognized as wanting something, which wasn't necessarily quick if the two staff were busy prepping drinks.  A "take a number system" would help, or a staffed till position.
      
      They have a really good selection of specialty pastries and good selections of drinks and coffees.  They have a food menu also.
      
      We had a large Americano, a large hot chocolate, and a flat white.  Sweets were a salted caramel chocolate tart and a caramel cake; these were excellent.  The hot choc was OK and the flat white was rated excellent.
      
      The big fail was the Americano, which in a coffee shop should be top notch.  Served in a big cappuccino cup, the first one was filled to overflowing (couldn't carry it without spilling) and had way to much cream (they add it).  It was pretty tasteless so was returned for another, thinking the cream had too diluted it.  This time, with less cream, it was still pretty tasteless - not as good as Tim's dark roast.  A coffee shops espresso/Americanos should be  marquee drinks!
      
      On the subject of cream, they have none at the small condiment station at the door for self-serve.  When queried they said something about no refrigeration?
      
      The staff were friendly and appeared to be doing their best, but that didn't offset the shop's shortcomings.
      
      Great pastries, but not drinks.  This is a neighbourhood coffee shop, not a destination.  We can't recommend it unless it's a matter of convience only.
