      For anyone whom this could benefit…
      
      I purchased the ‘after-market carbon fibre hood’ from a Chinese (Alibaba) factory for my 2025 WRX VB tS. They fit great and visually compared to Siebon hoods, they look identical. You do save coin, mostly because these hoods are not pure wet carbon fibre weave. They are actually a top layer of real carbon fiber cloth bonded to a fiberglass (FRP - fiber-reinforced polymer) or composite skeleton frame using wet-layup polyester or epoxy resin.
      
      I’m writing this post for those that are thinking to order a hood from Alibaba.
      
      I’d say go for it, you save about $1000, the wait time was 4 weeks.
      
      The only issue I had was that oddly, the hood-strut attachment points on the underside are off by about 2 inches.
      
      So I took the car to Grand Prix Auto in Edmonton, AB, and these guys attached the struts and reinforced the attachment area. Great job in my opinion, and the team there are more a family than co-workers. The pins are set through both a large washer as well as a rubber washer to offset the pressure point.
      
      I did order a far lower pressure hood-strut for this hood, I didn’t use the OEM. If you do go with one of these hoods, be sure to PPF it when you get it as to avoid rock chipping and sun bleaching.
      
      And obviously the guys at Grand Prix Auto are now well versed in making the adjustments!!
      
      Job well done!!
