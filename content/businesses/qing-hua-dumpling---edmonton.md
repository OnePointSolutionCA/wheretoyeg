      Fresh-to-order dumplings! Highly recommend.
      
      All the dumplings my friends and I ordered had that perfect, nice chewiness to the wrapper. The fillings were incredibly tender and packed with juices locked inside from the proteins (we tried the lamb, chicken, pork, beef, and seafood). A truly good dumpling needs to lock those juices in while maintaining a great elastic bite—just be careful when you take your first bite so you don't lose any of that soup!
      
      Our Flavor Standouts:
      1st Place: Beef Curry – Has a fantastic, nice curry punch.
      2nd Place: Beef and Coriander – The beautiful, fresh aromatics of the coriander work incredibly well with the savory beef.
      3rd Place: Chicken Curry – Delicious, though with a slightly milder curry taste.
      
      You get to pick your flavor and choose whether you want them steamed or pan-fried (for a small extra charge). We opted for steamed this time to really test the pure flavor of the dumplings, but I will definitely try them pan-fried on my next visit.
      
      Don't skip the Green Onion Pancake either! It’s nice and crispy and paired with an excellent, savory umami sauce. It’s a great departure from the puffy, doughy versions you sometimes get at standard dim sum spots.
      
      I will definitely be coming back here. Highly recommend checking this place out!
