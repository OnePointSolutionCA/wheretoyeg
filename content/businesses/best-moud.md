      An absolute hidden gem! I visited "Best Moud" food truck with my wife and daughter and we were completely mesmerized.
      First, the truck itself is a work of art - stunning black and gold Pharaonic design with hieroglyphics, King Tut, and the quote "Even Pyramids Started With A Single Stone." You can feel the pride and passion before you even order.
      We tried the King's Lamb Shank Signature with rice and the BBQ Lamb Wrap sandwich. Unbelievable. The lamb shank was fall-off-the-bone tender, the rice was fragrant and topped with cashews, almonds and raisins, and the balance of spices was perfect. The BBQ lamb wrap was juicy, smoky and so flavorful.
      Generous portions, amazing hospitality, and the best lamb I've had in Alberta. If you see this truck, don't walk - run! Highly recommended.
