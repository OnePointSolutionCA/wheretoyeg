      My wife wanted to try Nigerian food and she found Ola's Dining Nigerian Kitchen on Google. She was very excited about trying Egusi Soup. Our order came to $62.66 a bit too pricey, in my opinion. I drove 32 min from South edmonton to the north end to pick up the food.
      
      Upon receiving the food, I have to admit it was quite a disappointment, as the portions did not equate to the money spent.
      
      Taste
      
      We chose medium peper for the Asun, boy was that a big mistake. We are usually a family that loves spice, but this was ridiculous. The kids couldn't even try it. Even if you like spice, I would advise you to choose the mild if you actually want to be able to taste the meat. Lastly, it's not worth $20 dollars. You can go to the African market and get yourself a whole bag of goat meat and prepare it yourself (plenty of recipes on YouTube)
      
      The Suya was not bad, probably the only thing we got that we all liked. Again, it's not worth $15 it's more like 10$.
      
      Lastly, the main reason why we went there in the first place. The Egusi and pounded Yam. The Pounded wasn't easy to (swallow) and was a bit too chewy for our liking. My wife, my oldest daughter, my son, and myself did not really like it. The only person who liked it in the family was my youngest daughter, which was very shocking
      
      Will we order from here again?
      No. Respectfully, of course.
