      We visited Hathaway's Diner in St. Albert on Dec. 21st and was pleasantly surprised by the atmosphere, service and food.  Apparently, this location has been opened since Aug. 2024 and we had not heard about it for some reason...  It is a small location however we enjoyed it due to the smaller number of people there that day.  The chairs are too small and not very comfortable and each time the door was opened, cold air flooded in, so it made it very uncomfortable.  Our server was great!  She was very friendly and quick to take our orders.  Coffee was good!
      The food was prepared very quickly and served very hot!
      My wife had a Benny with a very thin turkey sausage patty, and I had a Corned Beef Hash Skillet.  The Turkey Sausage Benny lacked taste, but the homemade hashbrowns were very nice and the hollandaise sauce was flavorful.  The Corned Beef hash was a smaller serving than I was expecting, but it was flavorful.  There was a small slice of bread included that was not really toasted or buttered.
      Overall, I was more impressed with this location than the original location and will certainly go back again in the near future.
