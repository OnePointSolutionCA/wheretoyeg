      Who can resist the smell of fresh bread? As soon as you walk in you are greeted by the wall of breads and pastries and the smell alone makes my mouth water. The name of this place is well done as everything I’ve ever had here tastes so very fresh. Our first visit we had tried a couple loaves- the cheese loaf was a little on the small side but had a delightful amount of cheese; and a cinnamon raisin loaf which I adored and could eat all day long. I also tried the garlic bread stick which had a great amount of garlic flavor and I wish they had a deal if you buy a few of them. Our second visit here I got a seedy sourdough loaf- tangy and really really good; hamburger buns which were huge and oh so fluffy, along with some sausage rolls and some ham and cheese buns. Again would love to see a quantity deal with these smaller items but they were all very delightful, fresh, and tender bread.
      There is plenty of parking here as it’s part of a shopping complex, but there isn’t any bathrooms or places to sit. This is definitely more of a grab and go kind of bakery.
