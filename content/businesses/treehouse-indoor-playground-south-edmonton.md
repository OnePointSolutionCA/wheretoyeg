      We visited Treehouse South Edmonton on a Wednesday and enjoyed the 50% weekday discount. Expect it to be busy right at opening, especially on deal days — there was already a childcare field trip at 10 a.m. The space is clean and well‑organized, with cubbies for shoes and socks, a welcoming reception, and free parking. Overall, a busy, busy place with great variety and solid mid‑week savings.
