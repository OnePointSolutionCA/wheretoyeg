      I randomly came across this restaurant when I was extremely hungry and looking for the closest place with a great rating near my home. This restaurant came up with an amazing 4.9-star rating. Although it didn’t have a huge number of reviews yet, the score was so impressive that I decided to give it a try.
      
      Wow… this place is truly a hidden gem. The food was so delicious that I actually came back two days in a row!
      
      I heard that this restaurant also offers a buffet, but since I visited on Tuesday and Wednesday, there wasn’t a buffet available. However, don’t be disappointed. the regular menu is absolutely worth trying.
      
      The restaurant itself is very spacious, clean, and has no unpleasant smell at all. The server was extremely kind and explained the dishes very well, which was especially helpful because I am not familiar with Indian cuisine.
      
      On my first visit, I ordered Tandoori Fish, Butter Chicken, Butter Naan, and Roti. On my second visit, I wanted to try dishes I had never had before, so I ordered Chicken Chukka, Hakka Noodles, Vindaloo, Chilli Cheese Naan, and Garlic Naan.
      
      Everything was incredibly delicious. I’m not Indian, and Indian food is still quite new to me, but the Tandoori Fish, Butter Chicken, and especially the Chilli Cheese Naan were unforgettable.
      
      I’ve had Tandoori Chicken many times before, but I never expected fish to taste this amazing. If you order two fish portions, it becomes a little more affordable. One of the biggest reasons I came back was the Butter Chicken. It was so creamy with a sweet flavor. I dipped my naan generously into the curry and literally cleaned the plate because the sauce was that good.
      
      Chicken Chukka was also a new dish for me, and I really enjoyed it. Vindaloo was a spicy stew with a slightly tangy flavor, which was something different and delicious.
      
      But the biggest surprise for me was the Chilli Cheese Naan. I had never tried anything like this before, and I was amazed by how good it was. I’m Korean and I love spicy chili flavors, and this dish reminded me of flavors from my hometown. The naan itself was so flavorful that I almost didn’t need curry with it. The Garlic Naan was also wonderful with a rich garlic aroma, and the Butter Naan was delicious as well.
      
      I’ve had Hakka Noodles at other restaurants before, but this one had a stronger vegetable flavor. The chicken pieces in the noodles were slightly crispy on the outside, which made the dish even better.
      
      This restaurant also serves dosa, a South Indian dish. I’ve seen dosa many times on TV and have always been curious to try it, but there were so many other dishes I wanted to taste first. On my next visit, dosa is definitely on my list.
      
      If you are Indian, I’m not sure which dish would be your personal favorite. But if you are new to Indian food, I highly recommend trying Naan with Butter Chicken. You will not regret it.
      
      And if you enjoy the fresh aroma and flavor of green chili peppers, you absolutely have to try the Chilli Cheese Naan.
      
      For my next visit, I’m planning to try Dosa, Biryani Rice, and Palak Paneer. I can’t wait to come back!
