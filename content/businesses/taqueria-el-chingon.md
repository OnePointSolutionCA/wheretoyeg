      This cozy and warmly decorated spot is tucked away off the alley parking lot, it's more of a grab-and-go setup with limited seating along the walls and one small table.
      
      Our waitress was friendly and accommodating. Although there was a slight language barrier, the waitress confirmed my gluten-free requirements with the kitchen. The quesadillas use flour tortillas, fish tacos are battered, and churros are fried in separate oil from the chips, which are gluten-free. (Confirmed with the owner: Chips are fried at their sister restaurant in dedicated GF fryer).
      
      I shared the chips and salsa and a mix of Traditional Tacos (Pastor, Suadero, Carnita, and Carne Asada). The handmade corn tortillas were soft, thick, and absolutely delicious. I also appreciated being able to choose two proteins per taco order and not having to include rice or beans. Tacos were garnished with cilantro, onion, and lime wedge - which is all you need because the meats were so flavourful!
      
      For those with Celiac, I suggest visiting during non-peak hours so the small kitchen has time to manage cross-contact carefully. By 5:30 pm, there was already a line out the door, which speaks to how popular this place is. The staff mentioned plans to expand into a larger space soon, which is great news, as I’d love to bring my coworkers here once they can take reservations.
      
      Can't wait to come back! ❤️
