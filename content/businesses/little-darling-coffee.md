      We visited around luch time on a Friday.  It was busy but we got seats.  It's a quirky place with the small ordering area at one end of the Bread Love bakery.  I wasn't sure if the seating area itself was part of the bakery or the coffee shop?

      Recommended for coffee and a treat in the downtown area.
