      My Fiancé and I tried this place for the first time last week. An easy cautious order of their Shanghai noodles.
      This week we bought the Shanghai noodles again, the ginger beef, chicken balls, and green onion cakes.
      The Shanghai noodles are full of flavour, without being bogged down with excessive amounts of sauce. Their texture is great, and the dish leaves you full while also wanting more.
      The szechuan beef (ginger beef) is tender and flavourful. The vegetables mixed in with the beef were perfectly cooked retaining form and crunch while also being soft enough to not distract from the beef.
      The chicken balls lack the signature uniform shape you might be used to seeing. This is due to each one being hand crafted. The batter perfectly fried and layered to a perfect ratio between breading and meat.
      The green onion cakes are amongst the best I have ever tried. The fried dough is crisp without being dry, the green onion flavour stands out and is delicately mixed with a salty buttered finish.
      Overall, we are thrilled to finally have a GREAT Chinese restaurant close to home.
      If you are looking for a new chinese restarant, look no further you have found it.
      We will be back for more.
