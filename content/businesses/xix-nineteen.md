      Went to XIX to celebrate a birthday with a friend and overall had a good experience, although there were definitely a few things that stood out.
      
      I had the beef tenderloin, which was extremely flavorful and cooked beautifully. The reduction it was served with was honestly delicious, and the carrots were great as well. My only complaint with the dish was the portion of potatoes. I think I got about three baby potatoes on the entire plate, which felt a bit skimpy considering the price point.
      
      We also shared the crab rangoon dip and that was phenomenal. Super flavorful, rich, and the portion size on that was actually very generous. Definitely one of the highlights of the meal.
      
      My friend ordered the short rib. While the meat itself was good, short rib is already a fairly rich and fatty cut, so serving it with a deep fried onion ring instead of a potato felt a little heavy. I think the dish would balance better with some type of potato accompaniment instead. The sauce was tasty as well, though slightly sweeter than I personally prefer.
      
      For dessert, I had the mango cheesecake, which was really good, fresh, and light. That said, I’d describe it more as a mousse-style dessert than a traditional cheesecake texture-wise.
      
      The biggest downside of the evening was definitely the kitchen speed. We had a 7 PM reservation and didn’t receive our food until almost 9 PM. By that point we were absolutely starving, and eating that late unfortunately triggered reflux for me afterward.
      
      Service itself was good and everyone was friendly, but the kitchen timing really needs improvement. The food quality is there, though, and with a few tweaks I think the experience could be excellent.
