      We didn’t just try The Italian Centre Shop in Sherwood Park… we accidentally turned it into a full-day event.
      
      Started off innocent enough, split breakfast for two: a spinach twist, a bacon, egg & cheese breakfast danish, an Americano, and a cinnamon roll with nuts. Every single bite? Unreal. The kind of “why don’t we have this in Saskatoon?” moment hits you pretty quick.
      
      Naturally… we went back for lunch.
      
      Round two was the Monte Blanco and a custard-filled horn, again, both incredible. At this point, leaving without a box of treats felt irresponsible.
      
      So we loaded up.
      
      Back home, we turned it into a tasting session with friends:
      •	Lemon tart
      •	Blueberry cheesecake
      •	Ginger snap cookie
      •	Zeppole Di San Giuseppe
      •	Pistachio layer cake
      •	Florentine biscuit
      
      Not one miss in the bunch.
      
      My personal winner? The Zeppole. That custard had this orange creamsicle vibe that honestly caught me off guard, in the best way. Absolute knockout.
      My wife went with the pistachio layer cake… although I’m pretty sure I had to pressure her into choosing just one.
      
      The store itself is just as impressive as the food, rows of cheeses, olive oils, deli meats… you could spend a long time in there and still not see everything. And the staff? Friendly, helpful, and clearly hustling.
      
      Bottom line: this place is dangerous, in the best possible way.
      Saskatoon, we’re missing out.
