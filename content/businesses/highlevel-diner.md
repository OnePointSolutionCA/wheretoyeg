      (August 8th and 9th, 2025)
      
      Highly praised for their cinnamon buns by my conference website, I went here to get just that.
      
      Day 1 (evening):
      I realized that they run out of the buns early in the day due to high demand and that I should have called ahead and checked.
      So, for then, I settled for a Beetroot Dill Soup.
      Watery and flavourful. There was a patch of cream on top, it was sour. It enhanced the taste of the soup when mixed.
      
      I initially got the small one, and then it was so good that I again got a large one.
      
      -------
      Day 2 (fore-noon)
      I came again the next day, this time calling and confirming upfront.
      
      The Cinnamon Bun. It was soft inside, and cinnamon-sugary and crisp on the outside. The bottom of the bun felt well-roasted.
      We got a half and half of the cream cheese and caramel accompaniments.
      (a) Cream cheese was salted and sweet.
      (b) Bourbon caramel sauce was also good, we poured it on the bun.
      And that was very sufficient for the two buns for the two of us.
      
      I was so impressed with it that I got two more buns with both accompaniments to-go over our air travel to share it with a friend we were going to visit in Kitchener/Toronto the next day.
      
      Typical diner interiors and experience, and nominally priced food.
