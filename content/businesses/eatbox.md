      I don’t normally write reviews, however I had to with this place!
      My partner and I came in looking for a quick bite to eat, and wanted to try a place we never have before. We keep seeing raves about this place so we figured why not give it a try.
      We were greeted right off the bat and asked if we had been there before, and the owners son gave us some of his favourites to try.
      I’m usually a picky eater (yes like chicken fingers and fries over a steak dinner😅)
      We went in not knowing what the menu was, just seeing that other people love it.
      I had the lunch special, which was a Mediterranean wrap, with creamy lentil soup. My partner had the chicken shawarma platter.
      Both items were delicious, the portions sizes were great for the price point. The soup wasn’t chunky it was blended and smooth. The chicken wasn’t dry for either dish, and was super flavourful everything blended so well together.
      We got a chance to talk to the owner as well, she said she’s been cooking since she was 11 years old, she also stated that she made the menu herself and put things she would eat on there, and she would never serve something she wouldn’t eat herself. I love to hear that!
