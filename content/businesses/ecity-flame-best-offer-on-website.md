      We stopped in and were basically the only customers there at the time. I believe the owner came over to greet us, which was a nice personal touch and made us feel welcome. Our waitresses English wasn't perfect, but that certainly wasn't an issue and didn't affect the service.
      The food arrived fairly quickly. We ordered the spicy boneless ribs, a BLT, and cactus-cut fries. Unfortunately, the ribs were disappointing. They tasted like they had been cooked earlier and then reheated in a microwave. They were only barely warm when served, and a couple of pieces were so full of gristle that they were difficult to chew and I had to spit them out. I didn't enjoy them, and my son wasn't a fan either.
      The BLT was much better and was exactly what you'd expect from a good sandwich. It had a nice, thick, juicy tomato and tasted fresh. The cactus-cut fries were easily the highlight of the meal. They were crispy, flavorful, and the best thing we ordered.
      Overall, the service was friendly and the food came out quickly, but the disappointing ribs brought the experience down. The BLT and cactus fries helped balance things out, but I would likely stick to the sandwiches and fries on a future visit. 3 out of 5 stars.
