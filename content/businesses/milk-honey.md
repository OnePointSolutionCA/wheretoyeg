      Tucked into a church, Milk & Honey is surprisingly in a bright and open space. Mixed seating allows you to study/work at a table or get cozy in a cushy chair.       If you live in the Hamptons, Jamieson Place, Callingwood, or any other surrounding area, and you haven’t been to this cafe—you are missing out!
      
      Snuggled into the SAINTS church (it has its own entrance) this clean and bright, non-fussy cafe will surprise you.
      
      We had a “Morty” and a “Goat” sandwich accompanied by a delicious cup of coffee and a carrot cake scone.
      
      The sandwiches were top-notch made with delicious crunchy focaccia bread. The Goat with its hot honey sauce was our favourite.
      
      We also tried the Whoopie cookie. Both dessert items did not disappoint. The scone is so wonderfully nutty! The Whoopie pie is creamy and addictive.
      
      The staff, in particular, the owner, Lisa, are very friendly and eager to please.
      
      We live nearby, so this will be a regular spot for noshing.
      
      *The only downside perhaps is the $14 price per sandwich. This might be a bit high for anyone on low or fixed incomes, sadly. I say sadly because everyone must try this place. But everything is freshly made—so there is that!
