      My family and I were really excited to try Stone and Wheel Pizzeria after seeing all the great reviews, but unfortunately our first experience didn’t live up to expectations.
      
      We ordered and paid for three pizzas, but only one was initially made. When I asked about the second, staff weren’t sure what happened—either it had been forgotten or given to another customer—so they started making it then. When I was handed two pizzas, I asked about the third, and was told there was no record of it on the pizza making station ticket. After checking the till, it was confirmed that I had in fact paid for all three.
      
      By this point, about 50 minutes had passed,  our patience had diminished and the first two pizzas were already cold. I was offered the option to wait again for the third pizza, which I accepted, but given the delay, the multiple errors in the order, and the amount of time that had passed, I requested a refund for the third pizza, which was processed—though not very warmly.
      
      In total, it took over an hour before we left, and my family—who had been waiting outside the entire time—ended up with two cold pizzas and a pretty frustrating experience overall. When I said thank you on the way out, there was no response or acknowledgement.
      Really disappointed, especially given the strong reputation. Hoping this was just an off day, but it wasn’t the experience we were expecting.
