      UNA Pizza + Wine at South Edmonton Common is the kind of place that earns its reputation visit after visit. We came in for a relaxed lunch and left genuinely impressed on every count.
      
      The pepperoni pizza was the star of the meal. Cooked on a stone deck oven, the crust had exactly the right balance — crispy on the bottom, light and chewy through the middle, with a generous layer of tomato sauce and perfectly portioned pepperoni across the top. Nothing overdone, nothing missing. It is a simple pizza done with real care and quality ingredients, and that makes all the difference.
      The margaritas were equally well executed — fresh, balanced, and presented beautifully. You can tell the drinks program is taken just as seriously as the food here, which is not always the case at pizza restaurants.
      
      The atmosphere is warm and stylish, with natural wood tones, great lighting, and a patio that makes the most of a sunny afternoon. Service was attentive without being intrusive — exactly what you want when you are enjoying a meal at your own pace.
      
      UNA has brought something genuinely special to South Edmonton. Whether it is a casual lunch, a date night, or a group outing, this place delivers consistently. Highly recommended.
