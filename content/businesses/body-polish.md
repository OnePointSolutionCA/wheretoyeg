If a client is willing to pay for an upgraded service, I would expect some level of flexibility or, at minimum, an effort to provide a positive experience. Being told I either needed to return at another time or leave with wet polish was incredibly disappointing, especially from what is marketed as a high-end spa.
      
      The lack of attention to the overall client experience makes it difficult to see this as a place that prioritizes creating loyal, returning clients. The service itself started out wonderfully, but the way the appointment ended left me feeling very disappointed.
