      Queen Mary’s Market around Kingsway is one of the coolest hidden gems in Edmonton. The place is packed with vendors offering retro collectibles, nostalgic items, and unique finds you won’t see anywhere else. It’s the kind of market you can spend hours wandering through and still keep discovering something new.
      
      I was especially impressed by the Three Seas Nerd Market — they were custom-designing shirts right on the spot for anyone walking by. Whatever idea you had, they could bring it to life. Super talented crew.
      
      I also had a great conversation with the owner about getting some Dragon Ball Z gear made for working out. Awesome energy, amazing people, and such a fun environment.
      
      If you’re into retro, collectibles, creative pop culture items, or just want a great weekend stop, Queen Mary’s Market is absolutely worth checking out.
