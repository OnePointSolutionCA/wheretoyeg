      H-Mart is my favourite Asian supermarket in Edmonton—easily surpassing others like T&T and Lucky in many ways.
      
      What makes it stand out? First of all, the wider corridors make the shopping experience smooth and comfortable, even when it gets busy. Sale items are clearly labelled, which is super helpful when browsing for deals.
      
      They carry a great selection of seafood, fruits, and vegetables, all fresh and well-stocked. But it’s not just about groceries—H-Mart also offers a wide range of Korean kitchen utensils, cosmetics, and hair products, which are hard to find elsewhere in town. It’s like a one-stop shop for anyone who loves Korean culture and lifestyle.
      
      One of my personal highlights is their gourmet food court—serving authentic Korean dishes at very competitive prices, and best of all, no tipping is required. It’s a hidden gem for quick, delicious meals.
      
      The staff are always friendly and helpful, making every visit a pleasant one.
