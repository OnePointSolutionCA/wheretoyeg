      I’ve always enjoyed Barbacoa and chose it to celebrate my birthday this year, which made the experience all the more disappointing.
      
      The food itself was very good. The tomahawk steak was cooked beautifully, especially with the cowboy butter, and the complimentary birthday cheesecake was a thoughtful touch.
      
      Unfortunately, the service leading up to our reservation left a sour first impression. When I booked, I specifically requested a low table. About 30 minutes before our reservation, while we were already over 20 minutes into our drive, I received a call advising that the request couldn’t be accommodated. It was disappointing to only find this out so close to our booking.
      
      We were also disappointed to discover that the half chicken has been removed from the menu. One of the things that always set Barbacoa apart was having excellent options beyond steak. We had specifically been looking forward to ordering some of our favourite dishes, and it’s a shame to see them gone. The tomahawk could also benefit from a few more vegetable accompaniments, as it felt a little light considering the price.
      
      For a dinner that came to around $400, I expected an experience that matched the price point. While the quality of the food was strong, the seating issue and menu changes meant the overall experience didn’t feel like the value it once did and we ended up eating pizza when we got home instead of eating more. 👀😅
      
      I hope Barbacoa takes this feedback on board because we’ve genuinely enjoyed dining here over the years and would love to see it return to the exceptional experience we remembered.
