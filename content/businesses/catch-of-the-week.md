      Best and freshest seafood market in edmonton! The seafood is not just fresh its super fresh! The seafood is really fresh. So fresh it tastes like the ocean. Sushi made fresh hourly. The freshest salmon in yeg. Poke tuna and salmon is top notch.
      
      I discovered this hidden gem from my mom. She always goes here to stock up on fresh seafood. I was blown away with the selection as it is similar to the fish markets in japan. Nothing is frozen here since the items sold, are often gone after a few days.
      
      What caught my eye on my last visit was the giant king prawn and the fresh salmon. Both of those items were stellar and I honestly could not rave about it any more.
      
      They also have a quick stop catering place for for grab and go items like the crab roll and fish and chips. I really loved the crab roll as I do not know anywhere else that makes these fresh daily!
      
      There are tons of specialty items like oils, sauces, pastas, honey... even yogurt sauce. Most of the items are from Italy, france, japan and greece. Another great thing about this place is the little lollipops from france or juice from japan. There is always room for a little extra deliciousness.
      
      A must stop for any special occassions to get your seafood needs.
