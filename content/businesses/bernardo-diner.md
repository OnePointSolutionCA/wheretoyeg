  - name: "Chris Hewson"

    comment: |
      If you love Filipino dishes you'll love Bernardo's. I am lucky enough to have relatives who cook for the family and know the authentic stuff when I taste it, my partner and I both love the food here!
      
      My gf has previously ordered here a few times and I finally came along to try some too. Today I ordered the sisig which is to die for, it's so savoury and just a tiny bit spicy, the pork and onion compliment one another perfectly. My gf ordered the spaghetti and it is delicious as well, not too sweet and the hotdogs they use in it are really tasty too!
      
      For a small container of sisig and the medium container of spaghetti I think we paid about $25 (I decided to have rice at home, lol) and it's more than enough to feed both of us. As I'm writing this we are putting away half to finish later 😁
      
      The staff are also awesome here and patient, willing to explain the dishes, as it's understood that not everyone knows Filipino dishes. I asked about the pork and onion dish and they explained it was made with face and ear and would be very tender and fatty. This was a very good description and I'm glad I got it!
      
      Overall a great place to get take out or have a nice meal to sit in the food court with. 5/5 will be returning and recommending to my friends!
