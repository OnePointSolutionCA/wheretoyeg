      As a long-time member, I’ve decided it’s time to move on from Anytime Fitness in Fort Saskatchewan.
      The biggest issue is the lack of staffing on weekends. A facility that operates 24/7 should still maintain basic standards, and right now that’s not happening. The bathrooms are consistently unclean, and members shouldn’t feel like they have to clean them just to use them.
      There’s also been a noticeable decline in overall management. With the new manager in place, there doesn’t seem to be much urgency to address these concerns. At the same time, membership costs have increased, and members are charged a $30 “gym enhancement fee” twice a year, with no visible improvements. There hasn’t been any new equipment added in over two years, despite previous assurances that upgrades were coming.
      The gym itself is small, and it doesn’t take many people before it feels overcrowded and difficult to use. It gives the impression that this location is being left as-is, while other locations receive more attention and investment.
      At the end of the day, members are paying for a clean, functional, and well-maintained space and that standard simply isn’t being met here.
