      We have now been to this restaurant four times, and unfortunately, our experiences have gone downhill each time.
      
      Our first visit was wonderful. The food was great, the service was good, and we left very happy with our experience.
      
      The second visit wasn’t nearly as good. The service was poor, and we were disappointed.
      
      The third visit, we came as a table of six and brought four other people with us. Unfortunately, we were barely checked on at all. Our orders came out incorrectly, and the food was disappointing. One person in our group ordered crispy fries, but they came out as though they had barely been cooked. Even the regular fries were undercooked. It was especially disappointing because we had brought friends with us, and based on that experience, they probably wouldn’t choose to return.
      
      Today was our fourth and final visit.
      
      We walked in and sat ourselves. A young man brought us menus and water, and we could see him repeatedly reminding the manager/server that she had a table waiting to be served. Eventually, after trying several times, he came over and took our order himself because she was busy visiting with two other ladies.
      
      We later learned that the woman was actually a manager, which made the situation even more disappointing. The young man who ended up serving us, who was working as the bartender, was excellent. He was attentive, friendly, and did everything he could to make sure we were taken care of. I did speak with him about our concerns and asked him to pass the feedback along.
      
      Unfortunately, after four visits and seeing a steady decline in service, we have decided that we won’t be returning.
      
      It’s disappointing because our very first experience was so good, and we really wanted to like this restaurant. But good food and a nice atmosphere can only go so far when the service consistently falls short.
