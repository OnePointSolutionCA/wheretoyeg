  - name: "Bryanna"
    rating: 4
    comment: |
      Happy to see a Jamaican place downtown Edmonton. I got the Jerk wrap and it was great! The mix of greens on the wrap and the flavour of the sauce is perfectly complimentary to the jerk chicken.
      
      Also loved the vibe and the fact that they had small touches like a free newspaper for customers.
      
      My only feedback is there needs to be more jerk chicken in the wrap.
      I also wish it was a bit hotter- I love scotch bonnet peppers so I assumed there would be some sort of fresh pepper added to the wrap after asking for it to be spicy but it’s possible it’s more tailored to non-Caribbean people’s taste.
      
      The options for sides (for the wrap) were fries and Caesar salad but I say, stay true to your menu and do coleslaw or plantain, but maybe these sides are more popular with the locals.
      
      In addition, the visibility of the menu options can be clearer when walking in - I saw the sign on the street which drew me in with some options but didn’t know there were more options on the one pager menu that was at the ordering counter. Happy to see those options and excited to try them for next time.
      
      Happy to support this family business and will be coming back for oxtail, jerk chicken, goat curry and plantain. 👏🏽
