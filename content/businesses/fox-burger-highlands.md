  - name: "Emil Yim"
    rating: 5
    comment: |
      There is a quiet confidence in a kitchen that understands its identity and executes it with consistency. This visit delivered exactly that—bold, well-structured flavors presented without excess, allowing each component to speak clearly.
      
      The meal opened—unconventionally—with the dessert special: cinnamon dulce de leche bread pudding with vanilla ice cream, secured as the final portion available. The recommendation was delivered with enthusiasm and confidence, and it paid off. The flavors were warm and inviting, with the dulce de leche adding a rounded sweetness that complemented the spice of the pudding. While the dish leaned slightly on the dry side, it remained enjoyable and set an unexpectedly strong tone to begin the meal.
      
      The Brussels sprouts followed, offering a well-executed balance of caramelization and depth. They avoided bitterness while delivering a satisfying richness and a heat, reinforcing the kitchen’s control over flavor and texture.
      
      The centerpiece offerings leaned into the craft of the smash burger. The double Inferno smash burger with animal fries showcased a thoughtful layering of bold flavors without tipping into heaviness. Notably, the execution avoided the common pitfall of excess grease, allowing the seasoning and structure of the burger to stand out cleanly. The double Highlands greens burger, substituted with a veggie patty and paired with a green salad, demonstrated that the menu’s integrity extends beyond its meat offerings. The flavors were cohesive, and the combinations felt intentional rather than assembled. Portion sizes across the table were well judged—generous enough to satisfy without feeling excessive.
      
      That said, there is a small but meaningful opportunity for refinement in the smash burger itself. A slightly more robust patty or adjusted technique could allow for more pronounced edge crisping while preserving a juicy center—elevating texture and contrast without altering the core profile. As it stands, it is already good; with that adjustment, it could become exceptional.
      
      The space itself reflects a modern, trendy sensibility while remaining clean and approachable. Service was a standout: friendly, upbeat, and genuinely welcoming throughout. The staff’s ability to engage—particularly in highlighting the final dessert portion—added a layer of personality to the experience. Water was consistently topped up without prompting, a small detail that reinforces attentiveness and care.
      
      Value aligns well with the quality delivered. This is a kitchen that understands its lane and executes with precision, offering a dining experience that is both satisfying and thoughtfully composed.
