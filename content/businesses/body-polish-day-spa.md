      I received a Classic Pedicure at Body Polish as a gift, and while the shaping and massage portion of the service were fantastic, the experience after that point was extremely disappointing.
      
      I asked if I could switch from regular polish to Shellac, as that is what I typically get at other salons. I completely understood there would be an additional charge, as Shellac is generally more expensive than regular polish. However, after checking, the technician came back and explained that because a Classic Pedicure only includes regular polish, I would need to return at 2:15 for Shellac because they were fully booked.
      
      I declined to return for another appointment and asked to proceed with the regular polish. Once the technician finished, I was immediately rushed out of the chair and sent on my way with wet toes. Unsurprisingly, my polish was ruined almost immediately after leaving the salon.
      
      I have never experienced this at another salon. If a client is willing to pay for an upgraded service, I would expect some level of flexibility or, at minimum, an effort to provide a positive experience. Being told I either needed to return at another time or leave with wet polish was incredibly disappointing, especially from what is marketed as a high-end spa.
      
      The lack of attention to the overall client experience makes it difficult to see this as a place that prioritizes creating loyal, returning clients. The service itself started out wonderfully, but the way the appointment ended left me feeling very disappointed.
