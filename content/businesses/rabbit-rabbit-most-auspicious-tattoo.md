I changed my mind about 10 different times and, honestly, I was going in so many different directions with where I wanted to go with it that I drove myself crazy!!! She was very patient with me and my flailing and eventually, she guided me in the best direction to go and we came up with a solid design together.
      
      I had a total of 5 sessions to complete an arm sleeve, which incorporated many different nature elements, as well as covering up a tattoo and incorporating a pre-existing tattoo. I've seen shows where artists absolutely refuse to do cover-ups or they make it seem like such a big deal, but I never got that vibe from Kaisha; she was happy to tackle it on headfirst.
      
      While I did experience an allergic reaction to the medical adhesive, Kaisha quickly answered my frantic text late at night advising me on what to do and how to take care of myself. I am an extra kind of person (with extra sensitive skin) and she treated me with respect and kindness and put all my fears at ease. She further planned ahead for my next 3 sessions with that sensitivity in mind and suggested appropriate alternate healing options for me - above and beyond in my opinion!!
      
      I also used a tattoo numbing cream for my sessions.  Some artists will tell you "don't" or "no". While I did extensive research on this and there is really no reason why you can't use numbing cream, Kaisha was happy to work with it and further implied that you do whatever it takes to make your experience the best experience for you. Why be in paid when you don't have to be??
      
      The shop is a crazy, awesome atmosphere!  Cleanliness to a "T"!!! It is very obvious they take pride in their business and it shows!! The vibe is very friendly and laid-back. You get a feeling of home there and I was never overwhelmed with multiple artists working at the same time. If requested, they even put up privacy screens to make you more comfortable. They really do make it "
      All About You".
      
      While I recommend that you research your style and tattoo artist to make sure its the right fit for you, I do highly recommend that you check this place out and have a conversation with one of their many talented artists. Being that this is my third tattoo, this was by far my best experience yet!
