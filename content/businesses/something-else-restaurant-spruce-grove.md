    comment: |
      I’m by no means angry or upset about my experience last night, but I feel like I should give an accurate account of our dinner.
      
      As we walked in, we were greeted pleasantly by a waitress who quickly seated us.  The whole restaurant is carpeted.. not my first choice but whatever.. I’m not the one who has to clean it.
      
      It took a few minutes to see our server, despite it being rather slow, but that wasn’t a big deal.  We ordered dolmades to start, which came out very quickly.  For a Greek restaurant, I was surprised to see that the dolmades were just cold, store bought ones (the same ones the Italian market has).  They were pretty good, and the tzatziki sauce was also good.
      
      I ordered a blueberry iced tea, it was very good.
      
      Here’s where everything went south.. I ordered the vegetable stirfry.  Immediately I noticed that the vegetables were from a frozen bag (easily spotted with broccoli and cauliflower, and the carrots were cut the exact same way as frozen that would suggest a mechanical uniform slice instead of an imperfect knife slice).  The menu said there were snap peas in the dish, but were not present in mine.  I asked our server for some snap peas, but she informed me that they were “out of season”.   Again.. not a huge deal but don’t advertise an ingredient if it’s going to come and go from the dish? Or at the very least, add a disclaimer that some items are subject to market availability.
      
      I stirred everything together after removing the raw carrots from my stirfry.. (also confirms that the veggies all came together in a frozen bag, since carrots cook much slower than broccoli and cauliflower) but there was so much sauce, it was almost unbearable to eat.  It was literal soup.
      
      I slowly picked at the vegetables until I bit into a long black hair (I’m a redhead and my man is bald).  I told my server that I didn’t want my dish any more (I had eaten maybe 1/6 of it.. just a few bites).  She was very apologetic, and despite being very young and new to serving; she handled it quite well.
      
      Our server offered to send the manager over, but I felt it was unnecessary - I wasn’t mad.. I just didn’t want to eat what they were serving.
      
      I have managed several restaurants, and currently own a restaurant staffing company, so I think that I have a bit of experience to speak on this - if a table has that bad of an experience.. as a manager, you attempt to at least talk it out with the guest.   I know that I said it was ok, but if I were the manager, I would have talked to the table instead of letting an 18 year old “new to serving” waitress try to handle the situation.  Again.. I know I said it was alright, but as a manager, I would have touched that table immediately.
      
      My boyfriend ordered the haddock, and again; most of his plate was from a frozen bag.   His meal started with a tiny bowl of chicken noodle.. it was not home made.  The vegetables and fries on his plate were from frozen, although the fries were still relatively tasty.  I can’t say much about the fish as I didn’t have any, but he said it was ok.
      
      We asked for the bill, and the stirfry was still on it.  I feel like the waitress just didn’t know what to do.. but after asking about it; she removed it.  The bill was still $85 for 1 meal, 1 appetizer, and a couple drinks.  Considering the low quality of every dish we had, the prices are outrageous.
      
      If you want actual home made Greek food, go to Alexander’s in Stony Plain.  Just a short drive away and the food is considerably better.
