      Good value through Too Good To Go, but nothing particularly impressive.
      
      I picked up a Too Good To Go package from Blu Cafe Dim Sum for $7.99, and while the food and environment were nothing particularly impressive, the value of the package itself was quite good.
      
      I received steamed beef balls, chicken feet, and sticky rice—three dim sum items that are normally around $4.99 each, so getting all three for $7.99 was definitely a good deal.
      
      The quality was pretty much average Edmonton Chinese restaurant standard. Nothing stood out as exceptional, but nothing was particularly disappointing either.
      
      One nice advantage is that with Too Good To Go, you get the value without the usual expectation of tipping, which makes the $7.99 price even more reasonable.
      
      Overall, I wouldn’t go out of my way for the food or atmosphere, but as a Too Good To Go deal, it’s definitely worth considering.
