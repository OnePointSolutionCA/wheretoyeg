      Such a cute little tea shop on 124 st and Jasper Ave. They have a fantastic variety of loose leaf teas as well as unique giftware from local artists. Drinkware, jigsaw puzzles, embroidery and painting kits, pricey but worth it. Their teas are sold by weight and they encourage bringing your own containers although they do sell a variety of tins from plain to fancy in a few different sizes. We love the selection of Earl Grey teas and their greens are also great. Prices are significantly lower than Banff Tea Co  and Glenn's in Red Deer. The staff are lovely and knowledgeable as well. Give them a visit, you wont be disappointed. They're our go-to tea shop in Edmonton!
