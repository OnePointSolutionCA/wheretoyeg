      We stopped by Monacci Coffeehouse for a quick coffee and some pastries. On this visit we ordered a London Fog, an Americano, and two different flavours of macarons—one crème brûlée and one chocolate.
      
      The drinks were both very well prepared. The London Fog was particularly enjoyable: nicely aromatic with a smooth balance of Earl Grey and steamed milk, without being overly sweet. The Americano was also done well, with a clean, balanced flavour that highlighted the espresso nicely.
      
      The macarons were also quite good. The shells had a pleasant texture and the fillings were flavorful without being overly sweet, which we appreciated. The crème brûlée and chocolate were both enjoyable choices and paired nicely with our drinks.
      
      The space itself has a peaceful, relaxed vibe. The wooden furniture and warm design elements create a cozy atmosphere that makes it a pleasant spot to sit and chat over a drink.
      
      One small thing to note is that the seating is placed fairly close together. During our visit, the table beside us was watching videos on full volume, which made it a bit difficult to focus on our conversation. It might be helpful if the café had a small reminder about keeping device volume low, especially in a quieter café setting.
      
      Service was very friendly and welcoming. The staff member who helped us was kind and efficient, and our order came out quickly.
      Overall, a pleasant stop for well-made coffee and a small treat in a cozy space.
