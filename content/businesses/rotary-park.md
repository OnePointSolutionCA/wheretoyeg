      This is a quaint and fairly quiet park that sits in the north western part of Stony Plain. There is a paved walking/biking trail that goes around the circumference of the park. Park has 2 bridges, picnic benches, a gazebo.....a children's play area and public washroom facilities are also on site. This park has lighting. What I like about this park is that the town of Stony Plain goes all out in the winter months( especially for christmas) where it gets decorated with all sorts of outdoor Christmas decor with most of it being led lit. There is also a skating area that is partioned off in the south end of the park.   It's a winding river like skating trail that also is lit for night skating. It's constantly maintained and cleared of snow when it does snow. There are also temporary bench sitting areas to just sit and watch or when you want to put your skates on. This is a great winter addition for families. The only reason why I did not give 5 stars for this place is because the water spout/fountain that used to be in the pond is no longer there. At least I haven't seen it yet as of the date of this review. I don't know what happened with it or why it hasn't been there this year yet but that was a major focal point of the pond and park in general. Bring it back and this place will get 5 stars from me.
      
      UPDATE: I noticed that the fountain is now operating again. Noticed this yesterday( July 16/2022). So as previously mentioned I now give 5 stars up from 4. I don't know why it took so long aswe are now 1/3 the way through the summer but I guess that it's better later than never.
