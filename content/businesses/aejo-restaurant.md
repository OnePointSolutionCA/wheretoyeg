      Anejo blew my expectations out of the water! Looking at their menu online it sounded tasty 😋 and we decided to head over to Oliver Square to give them a try. Tons of parking and very little delays from the construction of the LRT.

      I had been wanting to visit Anejo for quite some time after hearing great things from a friend, and yesterday we finally had the opportunity to stop by for lunch. Safe to say, it lived up to the recommendation.
      
      We started with the table-side guacamole, freshly prepared and served in a traditional molcajete. It was fresh, flavorful, and absolutely delicious — the perfect way to begin the meal. I paired it with a Modelo Negra, while my wife ordered the Pineapple & Yuzu Ranch Water, which she confirmed was equally refreshing and excellent.
      
      For the main course, we shared the Classic 6x Tacos: 2 pork chorizo, 2 crispy fish, and 2 chicken tinga tacos. We also mixed things up with both corn and flour tortillas to try the full experience. Honestly, every taco was enjoyable, but if I had to rank them, the crispy fish and chorizo tacos were an easy 10/10 — packed with flavor, perfectly balanced, and incredibly satisfying. The chicken tinga tacos were also excellent and easily a 9/10.
      
      The service made the experience even better. Our server, Ashley, was friendly, attentive, and an excellent host throughout the meal. The atmosphere, food, drinks, and hospitality all came together to create a genuinely enjoyable dining experience.
      
      Overall, we thoroughly enjoyed both the food and the setting. Anejo is definitely a must-visit spot, and it’s a place we would happily recommend to friends and family looking for great Mexican food, drinks, and a welcoming atmosphere.
