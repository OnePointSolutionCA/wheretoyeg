      This is thee place to go in Spruce for a good sandwich!
      
      Yes, there was a wait—but only because this place is THAT good. There was a constant stream of customers, yet the staff were absolutely hustling and kept the food coming out quickly without ever sacrificing quality.
      
      While I waited, I may have accidentally started a bakery shopping spree. Every time something fresh came out of the oven, I had to try it! The standout was a piping hot, cookie infused croissant that was out of this world.
      
      The staff were incredibly friendly, especially Ashley. She kept checking in on everyone waiting, making sure customers were doing okay and making us feel genuinely welcomed. It’s such a small gesture, but it made a big impression and made my day!
      
      I ordered the Ploughman’s sandwich, and it was absolutely delicious and a fantastic value for the price.
      
      The café itself has all the cozy charm of a quaint European café with a warm country feel.
      
      Can’t wait to come in the winter time and enjoy a nice hot americano and curl up with a good book!
