      Burger Baron has always been a nostalgic favourite. This location is quite nice, and will definitely be going back.
      
      We ordered the Double, and a Double Dad burger, along with Onion rings and Tater Tots. If you love nostalgic old fashioned onion rings and burgers, this is a great place to frequent.
      The staff were very friendly, and made the experience quite pleasant.
      The Double burgers are filling and fairly tasty, I'd say 7/10, but aside a whole they're very good.
      The onions rings are coated in the classic batter, which is both crunchy and has that classic deepfry onion flavour. They're not like the kind A&W serves, which can be flaky and powdery.
      
      The vanilla shake is also awesome, thick enough to make it a shake, and tasty too.
      
      We used the outdoor patio, which is great all afternoon into the evening and gets lots of sunshine.
      
      Only downside was the washroom quality, which could have been cleaner, or checked more frequently.
      
      Parking is easy enough, but if you use the drive through it's quite easy to miss, or go through it backwards. Saw that happen twice on our visit.
