      Positives:
      
      The volunteers are very professional, kind, and helpful. Box office staff are so helpful.
      
      There really is no “bad seat” in the audience, even the back row has a good view of the stage.
      
      Sound and lighting is always well done and professional quality.
      
      The architecture and water features are so cool. The location is great with nearby walking paths.
      
      The venue is clean and tidy.
      
      Free parking!
      
      Negatives:
      
      Parking is very limited— especially during the work week and weekends. A lot of parking is restricted for city staff, local businesses, and the court house. Leave a lot of time to find a spot, or be prepared to walk.
      
      Ushers are unable to manage disruptive and rude audience behaviour (such as physically jumping over seat rows, swearing, and inappropriate yelling). At other venues, these “guests” would be escorted out.
      
      Accessibility is disappointing. Not all patrons are able to climb an incline ramp, and only 2 accessible spots out of an audience of 500 is not very inclusive. I saw someone with a broken leg in a cast get routed up the stairs, in the dark, because there were no accessible spots available. There was a lineup out the door and no folded chairs were out for those who needed them.
