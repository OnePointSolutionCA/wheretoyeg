      This review is long overdue....
      
      We dined at Mariachi back in March. It was quite busy, but we only had to wait 10-15 mins to get a table. Our server seemed new and not familiar with the menu at the time.
      It took awhile to get beverages, and to submit our order. We ordered the quesadilla and I wanted the chicken enchiladas. It took 20 mins or so to get our orders.
      My spouses quesadilla was okay. Nothing spectacular. I ended up getting beef enchiladas. Our server didn't come by to check on us for awhile and when I told her that I got beef instead of chicken, she said, "Well sometimes the kitchen messes up the orders." She didn't offer to replace the items (I still had 2 full ones left).
      The flavour for our meals was just okay. It's not traditional Mexican food or seasonings. If you want more traditional Mexican food, go 10 mins south of St. Albert and go to Smokin Barrels. Way better food!
      
      (I have chatted with a few other friends that live in St. A, and have dined here. They all said the same thing. It was good to go and try it once, but wouldn't go back. I know that I should have written the review when we first went, but it still doesn't change the experience. Yes, the restaurant could have dealt with the server, but the food was still not good.)
