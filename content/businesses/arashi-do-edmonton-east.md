      I first started at Arashi-Do East doing their stand up women's intro to self defense class, run by great instructors. This class led me to find my love of learning self defense and Brazilian jiu jitsu. I joined the women's only BJJ class here run by 2 coaches, two women black belts, and it was a blast to be in their class. To have not one, but two women black belts teach you is a privilege, the class was always a great time, the content fun, and the company even better. Having two strong women role models is really special, for both the little girls and the women who want to try jiu jitsu. One stripe later I also joined the adult fundamentals BJJ class, ran by one coach. This class helped me expand my comfort zone, working with both men and women on the mats. Between the awesome instruction and the great people, this class was always terrific. Second stripe on my belt and I was welcomed to join the advanced BJJ class run by the owner/professor. His instruction was always outstanding and the content always illuminating. This class was such a privilege to be in, his knowledge of martial arts is substantial, his teaching outstanding and his heart huge. Working with yet another black belt was always a great experience. Whether it was a class or open mat, he will always teach you something new and interesting.
      Training with all level belts was a great experience, never a boring class at this dojo. Through all three of these classes, I laughed, cried, and sweat alot. I competed in competitions and always had atleast one of the four black belts of the Eastside on the sideline, the love and support was remarkable. I made such good friends in these classes and always looked forward to go to the Eastside, eventhough I was always in for a good workout lol. More stripes and I began helping as a monitor in the kids BJJ classes. I loved having this special opportunity. Though the kids could be crazy sometimes lol, I never took a day for granted. I then achieved my blue belt here, not easily, but I did. I couldn't have done it without these incredible teachers, and all the beastside family. I will always be thankful to the Eastside.
