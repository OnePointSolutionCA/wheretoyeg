      We have been to TELUS World of Science Edmonton countless times and have always enjoyed our visits. I have been bringing my kids since they were toddlers, and now that they are tweens, we all still genuinely enjoy coming back.
      
      There are always changes, updates, and new things to see and do, which keeps it feeling fresh even after many visits. We personally prefer going during quieter times with fewer crowds. If crowds do not bother you, any time works well. If you do mind crowds, it is worth checking their busy times, as it can get extremely busy, chaotic, and very loud.
      
      The Zeidler Dome is always a hit, regardless of what is playing. Be sure to check the schedule in advance. Some shows are free to walk into, while others require tickets.
      
      The gift shop consistently has great items for both kids and adults, and the staff are always friendly, engaging, and fun.
      
      Overall, it's a fun place to visit as a family.
