      For our first visit, we spent $80 for 160 coins/credits and we walked out with two large plushies (24 points) and a goose (4 points) from the machines. The more you pay, the better of a deal you get.
      
      And I'd say what I paid was a pretty good deal, or otherwise I mean you can pay like $20 for 22 credits which isn't really worth it at all.
      
      The machines cost between 4-7 credits (about $4-7 if you buy credits 1:1 individually) and each machine's plushies have a point value between 1-2 (excluding one machine that costs 3 credits for small, point-less prizes), and you can exchange your winnings for bigger prizes up front, like larger plushies, Pokémon cards, a Naruto figurine, and Captain America's shield, for some reason lol
      
      There's also some gacha machines in the back where you can win Blind Box Kuromis and other small similar figurines for I think 7 credits per machine. There's a Demon Slayer gacha machine, too.
      
      I'd definitely recommend the place but I'm just a bit bummed out someone grabbed one of the plushies I wanted to trade for just before we could ask someone for it and we had to settle for something else instead :(
      
      I really wanted that strawberry Kuromi...
      Anyway, it happens, I guess. The place can be a bit pricey, but all in all, cool place, and definitely would come again.
      And the machines are extremely fair and very, very easy to win. Check it out!
      
      Edit: the place went to utter shit within a year. there's barely anything on the shelves, they rigged their machines, their capsule sanrio figurines are scratched up and it's all christmas plushies. disappointing.
