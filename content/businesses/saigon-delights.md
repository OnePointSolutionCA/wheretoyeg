  - name: "kim ga"
    rating: 5
    comment: |
      The decor inside had a nostalgic vibe, and it seemed like the restaurant had been open for quite a long time, but it was still clean and tidy.
      
      Special Beef Noodle Soup is a Vietnamese beef noodle soup in beef broth with medium rare beef, well done beef and beef meatballs. The bowl contained onions and spring onions and was served with bean sprouts, Thai basil and fresh lemon on the side. We could not believe how clear the broth was, but it was filled with a rich, savory beef aroma that was not too heavy or overly salty. It must have taken a lot of effort and time to prepare.
      
      We also ordered Grilled Chicken Vermicelli Bowl. The grilled chicken was served on vermicelli with bean sprouts, shredded lettuce, cucumber and pickled carrots. A bottle of their special fish sauce was provided as well. Mixing everything together gave the dish a balanced taste.
