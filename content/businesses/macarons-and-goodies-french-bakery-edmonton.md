      The bakery is located in a pretty sketchy part of town, and to get in you have to ring a doorbell and wait to be let in. I enjoyed everything I ordered, except I thought the macaron cream was very rich and was a bit runny.
      The bakery itself is really cute, the decorations are great, and they have a lot of options. They even offer high tea options for children, which is a nice touch. The menu has a wide variety of items, including homemade chocolates, cakes, and jams, so there’s plenty to choose from.
      The seating area is quite limited, so it’s not ideal for large groups, but overall there’s a lot to enjoy. They are also a Halal bakery.
