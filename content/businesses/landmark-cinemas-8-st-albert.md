      I LOVE coming to this Landmark Cinemas location. It is always clean and well maintained.
      
      I have only used the Premium section, so I cannot speak to whether everything applies to regular seating. That said, all seats are spacious recliners, and visually I could not tell a difference between Premium and regular seating.
      
      The recliner seats are huge and extremely comfortable, with adjustable headrests and heated seats. There is tons of leg room, and the seating feels very private with side and back walls separating each section. Each seat has its own side table, plus coat hooks on the side wall, which is a thoughtful touch. There is also a front railing instead of being right behind the seat in front of you.
      
      The incline in the theatre is excellent. No one will ever block your view, whether you are short or sitting behind a tall person. When seated, you can barely see other people at all, which makes the experience feel even more private.
      
      The screen is big, the sound is loud and clear, and overall the ticket pricing and seating options are not overly expensive for the level of comfort and experience you get. Plus, there is always a great selection of concession items. Just be prepared to come early if you'r buying drinks or snacks, as the lines move slowly.
