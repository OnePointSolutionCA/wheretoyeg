      Indian Asian Hut is a new grocery store in the beautiful and developing area called Aster in South Edmonton. This store is on the 17 street opposite Laurel crossing , closer to the 23 Avenue where the Medows Recreation center is located.This store is big and expansion plans are in place . It's located inside a shopping complex which also has a gas station, restaurant,meat shop , medical clinic etc. This is an East Indian grocery store and is very well stocked with plenty of fresh produce and other products from all over India. The shop owner was very welcoming. I noticed many items on sale and these were new good quality products at a very reasonable price. The stores location is great as it's visible from the main road on 17 street . They sell all East Indian vegetables,fruits, different kinds of rice, spices, curry powders, Indian snacks,frozen items etc. I noticed some beautiful glazed clay pots for cooking which were of different sizes and styles. They also have figurines of Indian Gods and Goddesses and prayer items. The price of the products were moderate . There is  wheelchair parking just in front of the store. The shop is well lit and welcoming. It's a great place to shop for East Indian products.
