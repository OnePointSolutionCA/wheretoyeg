      Good pizza that I was surprised at the location and building.
      
      When my friends first suggested this place, I was hesitant because the front store does not scream pizza place more dive type and probably bad pizza.Well I can put that perceptions to rest.
      
      The pizza is really good. I did not expect it at all. The pizza crust is amazing. It has  neapolitan flavour and texture. Remember Famoso's neoplolitan pizza, yes think that but Bravo does it 100 times better.
      
      1st time I went with friends, the service was okay not the greatest and I felt pizza was a bit salty. However the pizza crust was good. 2nd time I want to give Bravo a 2nd chance so I went with my Brother, it was better than the 1st time. The score be based on 2nd visit.
      
      Calamari is good with great crust from breading with the sauce.
      
      Spaghetti and meatballs is average nothing special. Standard pasta pizza and garlic bread was meh.
      
      But the pizza outshines everything. We ordered spicy chorizo. Perfect amount of spice from banana peppers. The pizza was not salty at all. Perfect amount of seasoning. Crust has perfect crunch and light and fluffy. Decent price for a large too
      
      My first visit my friends ordered meat lovers and spinach pizza.
      
      Dessert is nutella calzone. Perfect amount of sweetness from the Nutella. Crispy crust. Maybe a scoop of ice cream would be nice.
      
      The owner/chef worked and learned his pizza craft in Italy. Well done! The pizza is amazing.
      
      Overall I recommend anyone who wants to try good pizza to come to Bravo pizza.
