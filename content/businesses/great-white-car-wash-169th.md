      Been a member since day one when Great White opened and I’ve been on the monthly plan ever since.
      
      For $51, it’s been a solid deal. The wash is consistent  car always comes out clean and actually dry.
      
      Jeremiah and his team run a tight operation. Friendly, professional, no complaints there.
      
      That said, there’s something people need to know  the three standard memberships (Hammerhead, Tiger, and even the top-tier Great White) are capped at 12 washes per month. If you go over, your tag may stop working and you’ll likely get a call telling you that you need to upgrade to the next tier at around $90/month.
      
      If you wash your car often like I do, you’ll end up having to move up to that unlimited plan, which is a big jump.
      
      I just wish the company would clearly state on their website that all three of these tiers come with a maximum of 12 washes per month, and that going over means upgrading to the $90/month plan. That should be clearly shown on each of their membership tiers so no one is caught off guard.
      
      Still a good car wash overall, but the pricing and limits need to be more transparent.
