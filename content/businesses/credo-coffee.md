      They accommodate dairy free with soy, almond and oat milk alternatives! Unfortunately they don't carry gluten free fresh baked or meal options, but they did have some artisan packaged snack and chocolate bars that looked to be free of gluten at $8+. I tried both the chai latte for $6 regular and the vanilla bean premium latte for $8 large +$2. The chai was good 7.5/10 but the vanilla bean premium latte was exquisite 9.3/10 - it was so good I mentioned to the barista how much I liked it. It closed at 4pm, but it was Sunday. I would come back just for the vanilla bean latte honestly.
      
      Three critiques for the owner if he reads this...First gluten free options are a must, because many people are looking for this, And Second the tables fit my 15" laptop, but were too small to use a mouse, which slowed my work at lest 40%, and there is only 1 bar and a table (which was full and I didn't see plugs). Either switch to a round table or tables a bit (7" or so wider) so one can fit a mouse. Also the bench needs some cushions. Other then that great coffee, and great service.
