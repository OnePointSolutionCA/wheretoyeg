      I ordered from The Biryani Box expecting a restaurant that specializes in biryani to deliver a quality meal, but unfortunately the experience was extremely disappointing.
      
      The Chicken Biryani came with three large pieces of chicken (two drumsticks and a thigh), which sounds generous, but the dish itself was completely lacking flavor. For a restaurant whose name highlights biryani, I expected much better seasoning and authenticity.
      
      The Butter Chicken was equally disappointing. The sauce was incredibly watery and runny, with only a few pieces of chicken. Looking at the food when it arrived, it was hard to believe this was considered acceptable to send to a customer.
      
      The Sheekh Beef Kababs were overwhelmingly spicy to the point that they were difficult to enjoy, and the flavor did not make up for the heat. Even the Mango Smoothie, which I thought would at least be a safe choice, failed to meet expectations.
      
      What makes this even more frustrating is that the online ratings and reviews gave the impression that the food would be much better than what I received. Based on my experience, those ratings did not reflect the quality of the meal I was served.
      
      Overall, I am genuinely disappointed. The food was poorly executed, lacked flavor where it mattered most, and was not worth the price charged. I’m not sure how meals like this are sent out to customers. At this point, it feels like I would have been better off giving my money away than spending it on this order.
