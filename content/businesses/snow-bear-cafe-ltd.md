      AD Finding a spot that actually lives up to the hype is rare, but Snow Bear is a solid five stars for me. I finally stopped by the new location at Whyte Ave and it is exactly what the Edmonton dessert scene needed. It has that perfect vibe where you can actually enjoy treats that aren’t just sugar-bombs.
      
      The mille crepe series is the real deal! over 15 paper-thin layers of crepe and cream that just melt in your mouth. I tried the Matcha, and they are so light and fluffy it is almost dangerous.
      
      We also grabbed the previously viral pineapple egg bubble waffle because you can’t go wrong with a classic that is crispy on the outside and soft in the middle.
      
      If you are into more traditional stuff, the Taro Mochi Soup is a total comfort bowl, and the Rose Lychee cylindrical cake is almost too pretty to actually eat. The grass jelly soup is my favourite here! Highly recommend
