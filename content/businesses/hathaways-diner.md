      This place almost always hits for breakfast. I love the decorations, the staff are kind, and they have a great menu.
      
      Today we got the feature brisket benny and the feature cherry cheesecake stuffed french toast. Tbh the feature benny was a little disappointing. The brisket wasnt seasoned well and there really wasnt much of it. They also put mayo on the benny which wasnt a great hit with us. The french toast itself was really amazing, the berry compote is definitely necessary though for a bit of tartness. I didnt really taste the cheesecake but it was still good. One thing i’ve noticed, and I dont know if this applies to everything, when it comes to cream fillings (such as in the pies and french toast) or whipped cream, it kind of tastes like cool whip with flavouring.
      
      My only other criticisms would be that the taller tables (in the front and back corner) are kind of hard to sit in. The seat is way too far from the table so I have to sit at the edge which is uncomfortable. Also the juice is $5 for like a childs size cup.
      
      All in all, the restaurant delivers a pretty good experience but with all these minor food details, i do kind of feel like im being ripped off a little, especially since the prices arent cheap here and they are consistently busy (or so it seems). If the diner could address these complaints then i would say it was 100% perfect.
