Sometimes all we really need is honest communication, and when a restaurant responds promptly and sincerely, it makes all the difference. A small mistake like that certainly isn't a reason for me to leave a one-star review.

It's funny how we often forget to review businesses when we're happy with their service, yet it's so easy to leave a negative review over one small disappointment.
