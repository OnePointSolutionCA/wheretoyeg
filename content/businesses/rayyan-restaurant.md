      This is one of the best ethnic restaurants in Edmonton. The menu is varied with a mix of both authentic East African dishes and Afro-Indian fusion meals & snacks from several cCoastal-Island East African communities. The service is both prompt and consistent during multiple visits. The staff are very helpful for those new to the cuisine and they take time to explain the extensive menu. Some of the stand out dishes include saffron rice with either fusion chicken or fall off the bone lean goat meat. There are numerous healthy dishes including an incredible curried kale with chapati, refried beans in a delectable vegetable medley broth served with Ugali (an East African staple akin to a savory corn bread), and lean beef & cartilage with numerous types of Somali style pastas. There is various fish dishes cooked in either with coconut curry, fried whole fish or baked Salmon dish served with steamed basmati rice pilaf. Do not miss their incredible snacks stemming from the Somali/Kenyan/Tanzanian coast including to die for Beef Samosas, Indian Fusion Bajiyas, Coconut infused Mahamri treats, and other varied dishes that go well with spicy chai-tea infused with Chai masala spices. Their portions are enough to feed more than one person, and at a  price point that is refreshingly attainable in today's hyperinflated pricing for eating out. Do not miss on Rayyan's dinning experience both for lunch & dinner.         🤌🏼🤌🏼🤌🏼🤌🏼🤌🏼!
