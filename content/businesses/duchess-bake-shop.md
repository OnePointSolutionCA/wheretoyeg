      We were visiting Edmonton and were looking for the best place to try macarons. Based on chatgoodle reviews, Duchesse Cafe was one of the top recommendations.
      
      The café itself is absolutely beautiful. The interior is stunning, and we really loved the atmosphere. We came close to closing time, so most of the pastry display was already half empty. However, the macaron display was still more than half full, so we felt confident buying a selection.
      
      Unfortunately, our expectations weren’t met. Out of the 12 macarons we purchased, only one tasted truly fresh. It even made us wonder if some of them had been from the previous day. The fresh macaron had a texture that reminded us more of a cookie than a traditional French macaron.
      
      Would we come back specifically for the macarons? Probably not.
      
      We don’t want to lower the overall rating because the interior and atmosphere are genuinely outstanding, so we’re leaving 4 stars. But if we were rating the macarons alone, we’d probably give them 2 stars. We were just a little disappointed because our expectations were much higher for such price.

      Dutchess Bake Shop is such a wonderful experience from the moment you walk through the door.
      
      I recently visited with my brother and sister, and our plan was simple: order a variety of pastries and desserts so we could all share and sample as much as possible. It was the perfect way to experience everything they have to offer.
      
      Of course, no visit is complete without macarons! We tried the Pistachio and Salted Caramel, and while the Pistachio was my personal favourite, my sister absolutely loved the Salted Caramel. You have to try one, maybe tree! (No judgement here)
      
      The cakes and desserts were truly incredible.
      The Key Lime Pie tasted like summer in every bite… fresh, bright, and so delicious that you immediately wanted another slice. The Lemon Meringue was pure comfort and nostalgia. It had the perfect balance of tart lemon, sweet meringue, and buttery pastry. It was one of those desserts that makes you close your eyes for a moment just to enjoy it.
      
      For the chocolate lovers, the chocolate cake is a must. Rich, indulgent, decadent, and deeply flavourful, it’s everything you’d hope for in a chocolate dessert. Pair it with a fresh coffee or a beautiful latte, and you’ve got the perfect treat.
      
      Beyond the food, the bakery itself is gorgeous. The décor is elegant, charming, and inviting with a touch of old-world sophistication. Whether you’re catching up with siblings after lunch, meeting friends after dinner, or simply satisfying a sweet tooth, Dutchess Bakery is a place worth visiting.
      
      Everything we tried was exceptional, and we left already talking about what we’d order on our next visit. Highly recommended! ❤️✨
