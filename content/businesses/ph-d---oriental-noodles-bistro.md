      If anyone asks me where to get the best pho in Edmonton, this is probably the one place I hesitate to recommend... simply because I don't want it to become even busier than it already is!
      
      To avoid the crowds, I usually visit during the quieter hours – after lunch and before the dinner rush. It's the perfect time to enjoy a bowl of pho in peace.
      
      What makes Pho Du stand out? First of all, the prices already include tax, and there's no tipping required, which is becoming increasingly rare these days. More importantly, the portions are incredibly generous, with plenty of quality beef in every bowl.
      
      But the real star is the broth. Rich, aromatic, perfectly balanced, and clearly simmered with care for hours. Every spoonful is packed with flavour without being overly salty or greasy. It's the kind of broth that keeps you coming back again and again.
      
      I've tried pho at many places across Edmonton over the years, and in my opinion, Pho Du is still the best. Great food, fair pricing, generous portions, and consistently outstanding quality.
      
      Hopefully this little secret doesn't become too popular... because I'd still like to be able to grab a bowl without waiting forever!
