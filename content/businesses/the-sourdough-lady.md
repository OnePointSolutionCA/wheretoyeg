      Chantal is the Michelangelo of sourdough! This is honestly the best sourdough I’ve ever had. It was the perfect balance of fluffy and dense, with just the right amount of tang. We bought the smoked cheddar, jalapeño cheddar and bacon and herb, and all are extremely flavorful and rich. Chantal is extremely generous with the toppings; and you can see thick bands of cheddar running through the bread. This sourdough is so good it managed to displace spaghetti as the star of our dinner. 4 hours later we’re still talking about it.
      She also makes incredible cookies and cinnamon buns, and even sells brownie dough and pancake mix! This place gets all the bonus points for using organic, non-GMO flour. So often I feel unwell after eating commercial store-bought bread because of all the preservatives but I felt great after eating this.
      The bakery itself is beautiful, being located in a lovely historic building that adds to the cozy feel of the place.
      If you haven’t been yet, all I can say is this place is 100% worth a trip.
