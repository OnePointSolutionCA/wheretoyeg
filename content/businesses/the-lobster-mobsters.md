      I had the Duo Roll with both lobster and crab, and it was nothing short of amazing. The seafood was incredibly fresh, and the amount of meat packed into the roll was impressive. The toasted golden bun, brushed with garlic butter, tied everything together perfectly.
      
      This is strictly a take-out spot, as Lobster Mobster operates out of a fish market rather than a sit-down restaurant—but that only adds to the charm. At $25 for the roll, I found it absolutely reasonable for the quality and portion size. Being from Cape Breton, Nova Scotia, I can say with confidence that this is exactly what you’d pay back home, if not more.
      
      I also picked up some delicious fish cakes and smoked mackerel from the market, both of which were top-notch. Overall, Lobster Mobster delivers big on flavor and freshness. I’ll definitely be back!
