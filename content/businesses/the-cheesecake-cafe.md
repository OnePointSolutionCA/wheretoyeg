      Went here with friends after not going to one in so many years. Brought a bit of nostalgia as this used to be a popular spot in my pre-teen/teen era.
      The cheesecakes on display look great and food was delicious. I ate the lettuce wrap that was with chicken, peanuts, carrots, edamame and red onions in a nice peanut sauce on a bed of fried wonton strips.  Served with a few pieces of romaine lettuce. It could have definitely used a bit more lettuce but it was still very tasty. I was also randomly craving mashed potatoes with gravy. The potatoes were great but the gravy didn’t do it for me. It was almost too salty. Hard to describe but I’m not really a gravy person, so maybe my preference shouldn’t be considered lol
      
      My friends ordered an Alfredo pasta with cavatappi noodles, fish tacos with a caesar salad and a beef burger with chips. Everything was tasty and well prepared.  My drink was a seltzer with a dash of 7up and berry burst flavour. I honestly could have done without the 7up as the flavouring alone was suffice.
      By the time it was dessert, we were too full and we took it go. German chocolate was delicious! I wish I’d taken a photo lol
      
      I would definitely go back! Parking is plentiful but I would definitely consider making reservations for Friday, Saturday and Sundays. Staff was great. 7pm and onward is a good time to beat the rush and enjoy the ambiance and get a seat .
      
      Thanks for having us! We will be back!
