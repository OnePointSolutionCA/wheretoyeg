      If there was an option for 6 stars I’d choose that!! This place is PHENOMENAL.
      
      My husband and I chose Acero for our anniversary dinner, and it exceeded every expectation we had. We truly stumbled upon it by chance and gave it a gamble and we were beyond impressed.
      
      From the moment we walked in, the service was outstanding—friendly, attentive, and genuinely welcoming. Every staff member made us feel like valued guests, which made our evening even more special. The restaurant is small but not cramped. If you’re set on getting a table I’d recommend a reservation, but we lucked out and there were only two other tables in use for our visit.
      
      The food was absolutely incredible. We started with the mussels in a creamy white wine broth, and when I asked for something spicy, the kitchen brought out their bomba chili spread. It was a complete game changer and one of the most flavorful things I’ve tasted in a long time. Every dish we ordered was beautifully prepared, full of flavor, and clearly made with care and attention to detail.
      
      What impressed us even more was the value. We typically spend significantly more at larger chain steakhouses, yet the quality, creativity, and overall dining experience at Acero were on an entirely different level. We left amazed that such exceptional food and service came at such a reasonable price.
      
      It’s rare to find a restaurant that delivers on atmosphere, service, quality, and value all at once, but Acero did exactly that. We will absolutely be back and would highly recommend it to anyone looking for a memorable dining experience in Sherwood Park. If you want a meal you won’t forget, and support local in the process….this is for you!!
