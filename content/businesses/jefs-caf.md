      Jef’s Café was pointed out to me by the bookstore a couple of doors down.  It’s a cute little spot on Beaumont’s main street, an area with a unique old-world character that feels a little more European than what you’d expect to find in a small Alberta community.  Despite the steady traffic, there’s a relaxed feel to the area.
      
      I stopped in for a latte, and the price was on par with what you’d pay at one of the higher-end chain coffee shops.  The difference is that this is an independently owned business serving the local community.  Located right beside the well-known Chartier restaurant, it’s a nice place to slow down, grab a coffee, and enjoy the character of downtown Beaumont.
