This was my second time visiting Spring Hotpot, and I honestly really enjoyed it. The food was just as good as the first visit—so consistent, every time. From the moment I walked in, the service was very friendly. The staff greeted me with big smiles and I didn’t have to wait, which made the whole experience feel effortless and welcoming.
      
      The decor is gorgeous too. The nice grass and flowers throughout the space really brightened everything up and made the whole restaurant feel inviting, fresh, and full of life. It’s the kind of setting that instantly makes you want to relax and dig in.
      
      And the selection is great—there’s a wide variety of meats, veggies, and seafood (obsessed with their fish balls and shell fish) to choose from. The soup broths are especially yummy and rich, and my personal favorite is the Tom Yum. Some of my friends mentioned that the broth might be a bit on the lighter side, but I thought the flavoring was just perfect—balanced, delicious, and not overpowering.
      
      I also asked about the broths, and they shared that all of them are made from natural ingredients with no artificial ingredients, which makes it feel like a healthier choice. They really tasted fresh, and you can tell there’s care put into what they serve.
      
      Since it’s still a newer restaurant, I could see they were super busy—and even when new items came out, people grabbed them quickly. I noticed the staff had to continuously refill items, but they still moved fast and kept everything running smoothly as best they could. I could tell they were working hard, and honestly that’s to be expected when a place is this popular.
      
      Overall, I had an pleasant experience and I can see how things will only improve as they continue to settle in. Even though the crowd is still huge, I’d say give them a little time to smoothen their operations—and it’s going to be even better.
      
      I love Spring Hotpot, and I’ll definitely be back.
