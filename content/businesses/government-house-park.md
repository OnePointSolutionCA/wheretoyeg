      Government House Park – a great little inner-city spot right by the River Valley.  Perfect for a picnic.  They’ve got rotating little fire pits, plenty of benches, and what some people call the “crazy tree” (kids love to crawl all over that little structure).
      
      Our neighbours were a few families having pizza, while we cooked a couple of hot dogs and then lay back on the blanket staring at our little fire.  What a nice slice of heaven in the downtown city core—you can even see the skyline in the distance, and you don’t notice the traffic noise going by.
      
      Just another civic treat.  Thank you, Edmonton.
